# ISO/IEC/IEEE documents — scope only

**Read when:** citing ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514, 29148 or 42010, or IEEE 1016, or when a request expects one
of them to be followed.

**Source labels:** this family was classified only from its public scope and summary. The normative
bodies were not read, so nothing here is a rule of those documents.

## What the scope says (as captured)

- ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514: provides requirements for the structure, content and format of information for
  users of software. [XS-C38]
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148: defines information items for requirements engineering. [XS-C38]
- ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010: covers stakeholders, concerns, viewpoints, views, and decisions and their
  rationale. [XS-C38]
- IEEE 1016: describes the content and organization of software design descriptions. [XS-C38]
- Documentation spans the whole life cycle, including maintenance of the design, according to the
  26514 and 29148 scopes. [XS-C39] The position of maintenance in the process is inference.

## Limits

- The normative text, tables of contents and chapter structure were not read. [XS-C38 limit]
- The 42010 versions are described inconsistently across captures: the concept diagram is from the
  2011 version and the definitions from the 2022 version. State the version you cite. [XS-C38]
- IEEE 1016 is recorded with an inactive-reserved status in the captured text. Do not treat it as a
  current standard on that evidence. [XS-C38]
- Do not claim conformance to any of these documents.
