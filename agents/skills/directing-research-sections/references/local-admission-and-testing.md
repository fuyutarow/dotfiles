# Local admission and testing — SOLE owner of one-mandate selection

This file owns candidate/test admission inside one current `SECTION_MANDATE` and its
`SECTION_CHARTER`. Programme problem selection and portfolio value belong to
`supervising-research-programmes`. Candidate genesis belongs to `forging-novel-theses`. Those seams agree
in substance; do not diff for byte identity.

## Bounded live ledger before admission

The exact progress and lease rules are the sole subject of `scientific-progress.md`. This file only
chooses a local candidate/test and its route.

1. Record every received candidate, generated proposal, cross-domain mapping attempt, and explicit non-candidate in
   the live `SECTION_STATE` ledger. Retain rejected and loser records.
2. Deduplicate semantic duplicates; retain merged IDs, source locators, and merge reason.
3. Require exact Goal→Programme→Issue→Mandate→Charter→Grounding lineage, a current grounding fence,
   grounding `KNOWN_RESULT` boolean plus disposition (`NOVEL_GAP|REGISTERED_REPLICATION|KNOWN_DUPLICATE|RETRACTION_RISK`), value class, and
   dependency/dominance state before selection.
4. At most one candidate/test may be admitted at a time across search, build, execution, learning, and commit. Before another candidate cycle, the admitted work
   must reach a terminal receipt, learner proposal, and separate Director commit. That commit alone reopens
   the WIP slot; no unrelated section or programme wave may delay it.
5. Searchers may supply packets/evidence; builders may supply executable specifications. Neither admits,
   updates state, executes, observes, or obtains learning authority.

No frozen batch denominator is required. A human method input remains `AUTHORITY: NONE` under the
visibility contract; it is not permission to select the most convenient proposal.

## Separate admission axes

Score every surviving candidate separately on these axes: consequence, discriminability, feasibility,
novelty delta, and bounded loss. Do not replace them with one scalar product. Record the basis and the
loser reason for every `REJECT` or `DEFER` entry. `ADMIT` means only that the candidate may receive a
prospective local run; it is not a programme adoption or truth claim.

`KNOWN_DUPLICATE` is rejected unless its declared value class is `REGISTERED_REPLICATION` with a distinct
discriminator. A blocked downstream action is `DEFER`, never a convenient substitute admitted under
spare capacity. First action is one minimal existence/discriminator test; a fixed parameter/seed sweep is
execution, not search, and full sweep/scale/GPU port needs a `DECISION=COMMIT`,
`SCALE_RELEASE=ESCALATED_CONFIRMATION` receipt-linked Director release inside a
Programme-released mandate. When both blocks apply, both releases are required.

Candidate genesis through `forging-novel-theses` and search may start only after the current Charter and a
Grounding packet that exact-joins it establish Goal→Programme→Issue→Mandate→Charter→Grounding lineage.

## Cross-domain mapping disposition

Keep every `CANDIDATE` and `MAPPING-BREAK` from a cross-domain mapping attempt in the live ledger. Record a
local `MAPPING_TRANSFER_DISPOSITION` for each attempt: `ADMIT`, `REJECT`, `DEFER`, or `COVERAGE_GAP`, with the
separate axes and loser reason. Source-domain success is not target evidence. A mapping break is a
preserved negative result, never a weak candidate.

This is not the lateral learning bus. A received `SECTION_TRANSFER_PACKET` first follows
`cross-section-learning.md`; only a distinct recipient transfer commit may turn an adopted delta into a
local prior or candidate/test input. That input then passes the ordinary axes here and the WIP=1 gate.

## One-bet routing cut

Apply this cut only after local admission identifies one candidate/test tree.

| Condition | Route | Required return |
|---|---|---|
| Load-bearing and expensive or hard to reverse, including costly downstream exposure | `acting-on-hypotheses` | receipt joined to this charter/mandate |
| Deterministic, known-method, bounded, and reversible with no costly downstream exposure | plain executor | executor-owned `RUN_RECEIPT v2` |

Both routes stay inside the current mandate and charter. The Director issues the prospective intent,
then joins the executor receipt. Neither route grants a programme transition or permits a receipt rewrite.

## Admission record minimum

For each local decision retain candidate/test ID, provenance class, live-ledger status, dedup relation,
all five axes, disposition, loser reason if non-admitted, charter revision, and evidence locators.
`SECTION_REVIEW` may summarize a completed receipt/learning/commit chain, but is not itself learning.
It remains local; only a declassified signal or typed request leaves the section.
