# Lineage and evidence limits

This skill was originally distilled from idea-generation books and examples, but examples of successful
ideas are vulnerable to hindsight selection. The current generation engine keeps only operations that
can emit a transformation trace and a prediction.

The six RECIPES are an operational synthesis, not a discovered MECE taxonomy. The typed search
coordinates and the one-shot collapse threshold are skill-supplied control surfaces: they make coverage
inspectable and recovery bounded, but no cited study validates them as a causal theory of creativity.

## Evidence anchors

- Klahr & Dunbar (1988), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1201_1): bounded evidence for
  hypothesis-space and experiment-space search, including prior-knowledge and result-generalization
  strategies.
- Dunbar (1995), real-lab observations: bounded evidence that scientists use local/regional analogy and
  that controlled surprises can seed new accounts. It does not support distant analogy as the universal
  cause of discovery.
- Gick & Holyoak (1983), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0010-0285(83)90002-6): laboratory evidence that
  comparing analogs can support schema abstraction and transfer. It is not a study of real scientific
  discovery.
- Gentner (1983), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog0702_3): a structure-mapping account that
  privileges relational/systematic structure over isolated object attributes. It does not establish a
  source-to-target correspondence or validate this skill's packet schema.
- Gentner, Loewenstein & Thompson (2003), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.95.2.393): bounded
  negotiation/learning evidence that explicit comparison can aid transfer. It does not demonstrate an
  effect for scientific discovery, research documentation, or wikis.
- Holyoak & Koh (1987), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03197035): surface similarity can affect
  retrieval while structural similarity matters for use. It does not license selection by distance or
  turn a retrieved source into target evidence.
- Hargadon & Sutton (1997), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2307/2393655): a bounded single-firm account of
  technology brokering. It does not establish a causal or monotonic benefit of farther sources,
  brokers, or cross-domain contact.
- Dunbar & Schunn's laboratory work, [record](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/57c1f8xx), and
  [Schunn & Dunbar (1996)](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03213292): scientific reasoning can be prompted
  by more than explicit analogy. Felt provenance of an idea is not evidence of an analogical cause.
- Uzzi et al. (2013), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1240474): observational association between
  conventionally grounded work with a minority of atypical combinations and citation impact. It does not
  establish causality, truth, or candidate quality.
- Si, Yang & Hashimoto (ICLR 2025), [paper](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/ea94957d81b1c1caf87ef5319fa6b467-Paper-Conference.pdf):
  bounded evidence that large generated batches can collapse to many fewer nonduplicates and that
  self-ranking can be poor.
- Si, Hashimoto & Yang (ICLR 2026), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20803): proposal-stage novelty can
  decline after execution; generation must not present candidates as validated.
- Chen et al. (ACL Findings 2026), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2026.findings-acl.13): interacting
  agents can converge semantically; blind initial generation is an orchestration option, not a guarantee.

The broader primary-source ledger is owned by `directing-research/references/sources.md`. The claims
needed to operate this skill are summarized above so this skill does not depend on a cross-directory
relative path at runtime.

## What was removed in the genesis-only reforge

The following belonged to other owners and was removed:

- problem worth, why-now, and portfolio judgment -> `directing-research`;
- kill experiments, thresholds, withdrawal, and commitment for an expensive/irreversible selected tree
  -> `acting-on-hypotheses`; cheap deterministic reversible probes -> domain/plain executor;
- capital fit and runway -> no universal research-generation owner;
- multi-agent role/veto/acceptance mechanics -> `orchestrating-agents`;
- book-chapter-to-gate mappings and famous-success case proof -> historical lineage, not operational
  evidence.

## Interpretation rule

The engine is a disciplined candidate generator, not a validated causal theory of creativity. Its
artifact contract makes later scrutiny possible; it does not prove that a route will produce a valuable
thesis. The validator establishes a structural/mechanical floor only; it cannot establish novelty,
value, relation correctness, target fit, or truth.
