# Idea Factory broker: a bounded critical SoK

## Position

**Decision.** The research-harness maintainer needs an authority-safe way to turn a consented
encounter and a declarative Section Director need into a possible local follow-up. Within the
seven supplied external sources and the current repository contracts, the defensible position is
narrow: a broker may create **attention** through a declassified, non-authoritative, expiring
offer. It may not create belief, a candidate, a decision, or scientific credit. The evidence
licenses neither a generic innovation culture nor efficacy for LLM agents. [Y-DESIGN-1,
Q-LLM-TRANSFER]

**North Star.** Receipt-linked scientific **LEARN per wall-clock hour** is the only scientific
credit measure. Memos, offers, pulls, agents, tokens, fan-out, replay, and delivery are diagnostic
events or denominators only; none earns SEARCH or LEARN credit. [Y-NORTH-STAR]

### K1 FIT — review mode and coverage contract

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Review mode | Critical review / bounded closed-corpus synthesis |
| Question | What bounded mechanism can route attention without crossing existing authority cuts? |
| Corpus | Seven supplied external items plus named current repository contracts |
| Coverage contract | This SoK covers those supplied items and contracts only. Targeted recovery was used only for their stable locators. **No literature-completeness claim is made.** |
| Operator | Configurative synthesis of mechanism, precondition, consequence, and failure boundary; critical adjudication of folklore |
| Appraisal | Claim-level directness, exact locator, source type, causal identification, scope match, and transfer distance; no GRADE application |
| Output | An authority-safe operational position and manual/shadow-pilot unknowns; no runtime or efficacy claim |

The contribution is a critical cut: historical examples of memoranda, bridges, corridors, and
ties do not authorize a new global mechanism. They expose a small routing possibility and the
conditions under which it must stop.

The strongest in-scope counterevidence is not a rival broker design. It is the combination of
MERCURY's non-consensual author push, weak-tie limits for complex transfer, and open-office
channel effects: routing more material or making more people visible is not a licensed claim that
useful collaboration follows. [C-BTM-3, C-WEAK-1, C-EXPOSURE-1]

## Local responsibility cut

The following present-state constraints are design premises, not conclusions drawn from Bell Labs:

- The hot state is a typed durable event log; the Programme Supervisor is cold, portfolio-only,
  and may not schedule, interpret, or commit a live section. Directors alone admit locally and
  commit section state. [C-LOCAL-1]
- Human method material is quarantined as `HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT`, with source, time, scope, and
  `AUTHORITY: NONE`; its raw wording does not go to the Supervisor. [C-LOCAL-2]
- `SECTION_TRANSFER_PACKET` is a receipt-grounded, Director-committed learning path. A
  pre-admission offer is neither that packet nor a subscription/delivery/transfer-commit event.
  Propagation metrics have no SEARCH/LEARN credit. [C-LOCAL-3]
- Documentation may retain locus and lineage, but it cannot decide semantic admission or meaning.
  [C-LOCAL-4]

## Operational distillation

> **LAW:** An encounter may create attention; it may not create belief, a candidate, a decision,
> or scientific credit.

The only provisional path is:

```text
consented encounter + declarative Director need
  -> declassified, non-authoritative, exact-match offer
     (recipient-bound, expiring, withdrawable; body not Supervisor-visible)
  -> voluntary Director pull and record as HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT
  -> ordinary independent ADMIT | REJECT | DEFER
  -> candidate/test only through existing local authority

expiry | withdrawal | no pull -> terminal; no state mutation
```

The offer must not be a `SECTION_TRANSFER_PACKET`, must never enter the committed-learning bus,
must never be globally broadcast or model-ranked, and must not block source, recipient, or
unrelated work. A bounded concrete example/discriminator is a pilot hypothesis for deciding
whether to pull, not a historical rule or admission evidence. [Y-DESIGN-1, Y-DESIGN-2]

### Source-to-design inference limits

External sources can motivate a mechanism or boundary; they cannot assign target authority,
admit a candidate, award scientific credit, or prove broker efficacy. Authority semantics come
from the local contracts; the offer content and pilot are design inferences. In particular:

- MEMO metadata/routing supports discoverability, not priority or universal format. [C-BTM-1,
  C-BTM-2, C-BTM-4]
- The Kelly bridge supports explicit interfaces, not an automatic innovation law. [C-KELLY-1]
- Weak-tie and exposure findings constrain a reach mechanism; they do not validate LLM-mediated
  collaboration. [C-WEAK-1, C-EXPOSURE-1, Q-LLM-TRANSFER]

## Manual/shadow pilot: testable unknowns

| Hypothesis / unknown | Discriminating observation | Failure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in, exact, expiring offers can be delivered without authority leakage | Every offer has consent, recipient binding, expiry, withdrawal state, declassification record, and `AUTHORITY: NONE`; no body reaches Supervisor | Any Supervisor body visibility, global broadcast/ranking, or transfer-bus reuse fails contract conformance [Q-PILOT-1] |
| The path remains non-blocking and non-credit-bearing | Source/recipient/unrelated work continues after no pull, withdrawal, expiry, reject, or defer; event accounting awards no SEARCH/LEARN | Any acknowledgement barrier, quorum, global join, or credit increment fails [Y-NORTH-STAR, Q-PILOT-1] |
| Offers create useful attention rather than costly exposure | Record offers, pulls, expiry/withdrawal/no-pull, Director disposition, attention cost, and only receipt-linked later LEARN per wall-clock hour | Counts of offers/pulls/delivery alone are not success; no causal efficacy conclusion from a shadow run [Q-LLM-TRANSFER, Q-PILOT-1] |
| Concrete bounded context helps pull triage | Compare prewritten content variants only under a frozen manual protocol; preserve Director disposition and stated reason | Do not infer a universal template or alter authority from an observed preference [Y-DESIGN-2] |

These are pilot hypotheses, not acceptance of a runtime. The pilot must preserve negative terminal
events in its denominator and keep human-to-LLM transfer **UNKNOWN** unless its own prewritten
test supports a narrower updated claim.
