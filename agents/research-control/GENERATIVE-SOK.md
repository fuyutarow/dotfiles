
`VERBATIM` means this pass read the quoted sentence at the source. `ABSTRACT-ONLY` means only the
abstract was read. `SECONDARY-SUMMARY` means the row rests on a search-engine or third-party summary
and the primary text was **not** opened — such a row may not be quoted as if verified.

## 2. Headline position

Six findings carry the document.

1. **A synthesis is an intervention on its field, not a mirror of it.** Papers cited by a formal
   review "generally experience a dramatic loss in future citations"; the review gets cited instead.
   The exception is the handful of works the review names as bridges, which "become a shorthand
   characterization of the topic going forward and receive disproportionate attention." So the form
   question is not *whether* an SoK steers what happens next — it already does — but whether it
   steers deliberately.
2. **The steering the default form does is toward consolidation, and consolidation is the failure
   mode we are trying to avoid.** The same mechanism that makes a field legible ("render increasingly
   expansive and redundant bodies of knowledge distinct and comprehensible") suppresses its live
   conflicts. In large fields the analogous macro-effect is named ossification of canon.
3. **The generative shape is a conventional core plus an intruding tail — not less rigor.** The
   highest-impact work is "primarily grounded in exceptionally conventional combinations of prior work
   yet simultaneously features an intrusion of unusual combinations," at roughly 2× the hit rate. The
   correct move is therefore to **keep the claim ledger exactly as strict as it is** and add a bounded
   atypical surface beside it. Loosening the ledger destroys the very core the tail is priced against.
4. **The individually rational research strategy is collectively inefficient, and the gap is
   quantified in direction if not in magnitude.** The inferred default strategy is conservative,
   becomes more so over time, "is efficient for initial exploration … but is inefficient for science
   as a whole," and "increased risk-taking and the publication of experimental failures would
   substantially improve the speed of discovery." A generative SoK's job is to make the risky move
   *cheap*, because the evidence says the reward alone will not pay for it.
5. **A gap statement does not fill itself, and the honest prior is that most will not be acted on.**
   The uptake evidence available in this pass is weak but one-directional and no contrary result was
   found. Therefore the layer must be measured by retirements, must expect expiry as the modal
   outcome, and must carry a pre-fixed self-retirement threshold.
6. **The one artifact form with an uncontested discovery result carries a referee, not a reader.**
   The Common Task Framework's three ingredients are a public training set, enrolled competitors, and
   a scoring referee over sequestered data. It converges with the one architecture in this corpus that
   has an uncontested discovery result and whose gate is an evaluator rather than a conversation (G8).
   So: **the strongest thing an SoK can emit is not a better-worded gap, it is a task with a referee.**

Together these license one shape: **the signed position stays exactly as it is, and a second,
non-authoritative, expiring, test-bound, addressed layer is attached to it — anchored to the core,
capped in its atypical tail, and measured only by retirement.**

### G3 · The combinatorial shape of high-impact work

| Claim | Class | Locus |
|---|---|---|

> **GL4.** Anchor every opening to the signed position. An opening that cites nothing in the ledger is
> the low-conventionality quadrant, and the corpus gives it no support.

> **GF2.** Do not loosen the claim ledger to make room for the generative layer. The conventional core

## 4. What the corpus cannot say

- Nothing here measures any artifact form's effect on discovery. G2 measures attention; G3 measures
  the combinatorial signature of impact after the fact; G4 measures a simulated strategy space.
- Every effect measured on journal reviews is transferred to internal, agent-facing artifacts by
  assumption. The populations differ in incentive, audience, and permanence.
- Five rows are `VERBATIM`. The rest are `ABSTRACT-ONLY` or `SECONDARY-SUMMARY`, and the uptake lane
  (G9) — which carries the layer's strongest objection — is the weakest-verified lane in the document.
- Search was English-only, single-pass per lane, and time-boxed to one session.
- No lane covered peer review, incentive design, or the effect of preprint servers, all of which
  plausibly interact with every mechanism above.

## 5. Counterevidence against building this at all

1. **Curation demonstrably destroys attention, and this layer is more curation.** G2's mechanism does
   not distinguish good curation from bad; adding a second synthesized surface may simply bury more.
2. **The uptake prior is bad and the layer's own success measure depends on it.** If most rows expire,
   the layer's cost is real and its benefit is a tail event.
3. **The generative structure with the longest history — literature-based discovery — has not
   established a prospective track record** and carries acknowledged reproducibility problems.
4. **Every quantified acceleration in the corpus is a simulation in another domain.**
5. **The house already has a generative bridge** (`DONOR SET` → `forging-novel-theses`) whose coverage
   over `soks/` is roughly 44% by its own accounting. Widening the existing bridge is a cheaper
   hypothesis than building a second one, and this corpus does not rule it out.

The honest position is conditional: **a generative layer is defensible only if it is anchored to the
signed position, capped in its atypical tail, measured by retirement, and pre-committed to a
self-retirement threshold it can actually fail.**

## 7. Licensed and forbidden rules

**Licensed.** Do not justify generativity from the genre charter (GL1); declare what the sheet buries
(GL2); carry live conflicts as first-class rows (GL3); anchor every opening to the signed position
(GL4); cap the atypical tail (GL5); lower the cost of the risky move rather than exhorting it (GL6);
admit negative and failed observations (GL7); record non-adjacency as structure (GL8); type each row
by its reason class (GL9); point at the owned gap typology instead of restating it (GL10); declare the
axes before counting (GL11); publication threshold of one sentence plus a test (GL12); `AUTHORITY:
NONE` on the sheet (GL13); promote to a refereed task whenever a referee can be written (GL14);
referee and threshold fixed before observation (GL15); expiry is the modal outcome (GL16); measure by
retirement against a pre-fixed self-retirement threshold (GL17); carry the no-discovery-effect prior
forward (GL18).

**Forbidden.** Treating a synthesis as neutral, or claiming G2's measured effect for our own artifacts
(GF1); loosening the claim ledger to make room (GF2); reporting an acceleration magnitude (GF3);
stating or predicting a relation on a non-adjacency row (GF4); prioritizing, ranking, or scoring
openings (GF5); citing RFC 3 or the Bell TM as evidence of results (GF6); claiming the task form
accelerates anything (GF7); counting written, delivered, or read openings as progress (GF8).

**Ownership map.** GL1–GL18 and GF1–GF8 are the operating manual of `operationalizing-research-gaps`.
The gap typology stays with `systematizing-knowledge`; allocation and problem formulation stay with
`supervising-research-programmes`; target mapping stays with `forging-novel-theses`. No rule here is
duplicated into those skills.

**Declared residual (2026-08-06).** Delivery — consent, matching against a declared need, offer, and
pull — has **no owner in this collection today**. A skill that owned it was present when v2608.1.0 was
written and is no longer in the repository. Until one exists, the openings layer stops at *addressing*
(naming who a row is for) and must not broadcast, push, or deliver. This residual is declared rather
than absorbed, so that restoring the owner is a one-line change and not an excavation.
