# Frameworks — the coordinate system, and the "is HEXACO enough?" verdict

Grades: **[A]** meta-analytic / large-n primary, verified · **[B]** peer-reviewed single-study or
secondary · **[C]** search-synthesized, exact figure unverified. The framework is only **Layer 3**
of the method (method.md) — the "psychology of the stranger." Do not mistake a good coordinate
system for a finished read.

## The verdict up front

**Is HEXACO enough, or is something more effective?** — HEXACO is the right backbone and strictly
better than plain Big Five for this use; nothing "more effective" replaces it, but three add-ons
extend it. The order of operations:

1. **Backbone = HEXACO.** Big Five is the validated coordinate system everything is benchmarked
   against, but it leaves the exploitation/integrity axis diffuse. HEXACO adds the 6th lexical
   factor **Honesty-Humility** and fixes exactly that gap.
2. **HEXACO H is required, not optional**, and if starting fresh adopt HEXACO whole rather than
   bolting H onto Big Five.
3. **Add-ons by need**: Big Five **Aspects** for resolution, **Interpersonal Circumplex** for
   interaction dynamics, **Dark Tetrad (SD4)** as a focused overlay for cruelty/grandiosity only.
4. **Reject** MBTI / Enneagram / DISC / astrology; treat **GFP** as an artifact.

---

## Big Five / OCEAN — the backbone's backbone

**Uniquely captures:** the consensual, lexically-derived, cross-culturally replicated 5-dimensional
structure of normal-range personality (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness,
Neuroticism). It is the coordinate system every serious framework is validated against, and the
only one with a mature language→trait computational literature.

- Reliability: domain α ≈ .73–.80 (**Agreeableness consistently weakest**); rank-order stability
  r≈.69–.76. [A] Note the contradiction: raw stability *underestimates* reliability because it
  scores true trait change as error (Gnambs 2014) — treat ~.70 as a floor, not a ceiling.
- Text→trait convergence tops at **r≈.38–.43** — *stranger-to-friend* level, never spouse-level
  (Park 2015 [A]; Azucar 2018 meta [A]). This is the ceiling calibration-and-ethics.md §1 enforces.
- **The load-bearing weakness that justifies HEXACO:** all manipulation/exploitation variance loads
  diffusely onto **(low) Agreeableness**, which conflates "exploits others" with "forgives being
  exploited" (Howard & Van Zandt 2020 [A]).

## HEXACO — the backbone this skill actually uses

**Adds Honesty-Humility (H):** sincerity, fairness, greed-avoidance, modesty — directly indexes
willingness to exploit others when it is safe/advantageous.

- **H is distinct from Agreeableness, not redundant:** H×Big-Five-A corrected ρ≈.50; all of Big
  Five explains only **R²≈.18** of H — most of H is unrecoverable from Big Five. [A] (Single studies
  report H–A as high as .61–.70 because Big-Five-A instruments absorb modesty items; the meta value
  is ~.50 — a preserved magnitude contradiction, not a resolved one.)
- Reliability strong (HEXACO-100 domain test-retest .88, α .87). [A]
- Predictive: ~1.5× Big Five for counterproductive/deviant behavior; H the single strongest
  predictor; H (not A) predicts fair, non-exploitative behavior in economic games. [B]
- **H is recoverable from ordinary language and self-report-stable** — a normal-range lexical trait,
  not a clinical construct. That is why it belongs in an everyday profiler.
- Critique preserved: some argue H reorganizes Big-Five A/N rather than adding content (McCrae &
  Costa 2008); rebutted by Ashton & Lee (2020); the 6-factor lexical solution replicates across many
  (not all) languages incl. Korean [A] — so H is not a Western post-hoc add-on.

## Add-ons that earn their place

**Big Five Aspects (DeYoung 2007, 10 aspects) [A]** — two per domain (E→Assertiveness/Enthusiasm,
A→Compassion/Politeness, C→Industriousness/Orderliness, N→Volatility/Withdrawal,
O→Intellect/Openness). The highest resolution that is still empirically disciplined; use when text
is rich enough to distinguish, e.g., Assertiveness from Enthusiasm.

**Interpersonal Circumplex (Wiggins/Leary) [B]** — two axes, **Agency/Dominance × Communion/Warmth**.
Uniquely captures interpersonal *behavior* (what a person does *to others*) and is **dyadic** —
the complementarity principle (dominance pulls submission, warmth pulls warmth) lets you predict
interaction dynamics a solo trait score cannot (method.md L5). Maps onto Big Five E/A as a ~30–45°
rotation — it is finer-grained interaction vocabulary, not a competing taxonomy. Use it to describe
*how they act on others*, not as a rival trait system.

**Dark Tetrad via SD4 — focused overlay only.** The Dark Triad/Tetrad (Machiavellianism,
narcissism, psychopathy, +sadism) shares a callous-manipulation "D-factor." **Critical:** latent D
↔ low Honesty-Humility is r≈**−.95** (Hodson 2018 [A]) — for *general* everyday manipulativeness,
**low H already covers it** and is more parsimonious; adding a Dark-Triad scale buys little unique
variance. Use the Tetrad overlay ONLY for the two things H reads less sharply: **sadism**
(gratuitous cruelty / trolling — the strongest unique predictor of trolling, Buckels 2014 [A]) and
**grandiose narcissism** (status-signaling, materialism). Use **SD4**, never the **Dirty Dozen**
(psychometrically weak — convergent r only .34–.46; Miller 2012 [A]). Do NOT treat DT as identical
to low H: the r≈−.95 is argued "distinct-yet-correlated" (Thielmann/Hilbig), and incremental
validity over H is **outcome-dependent and unresolved** — preserve it as an open debate, do not
launder into consensus.

## Rejected — reframe, do not comply

| Framework | Why rejected | Number to cite |
|---|---|---|
| **MBTI / 16personalities** | type instability, false dichotomy, Big-Five-minus-Neuroticism repackaging, ~1 % criterion validity | ~**50 %** get a different type on ≥1 scale in 5 weeks (McCarley & Carskadon 1983); traits are continuous, not bimodal (McCrae & Costa 1989); leadership R²≈.01 (Zárate-Torres 2023) [A/B] |
| **Enneagram** | mixed/scarce reliability & validity; factor analyses find <9 factors; no criterion validity | systematic review of 104 samples (Hook 2021) [B] |
| **DISC** | four dimensions not psychometrically independent (Big Five blends); ipsative format blocks between-person comparison | Martinussen 2003 [B] |
| **Astrology / any birth-based type** | no mechanism, no validity; pure Barnum | — |
| **GFP ("general factor of personality")** | mostly evaluative/self-presentation bias, not a real trait; general-factor saturation ωh≈.37 vs .74 for cognitive g; neutral item-wording *prevents* GFP extraction | Revelle & Wilt 2013 [A/B] — treat any "one big factor" reading as an impression-management/halo signal |

**How to reframe an MBTI/type request** (do not refuse coldly — redirect to something better):
acknowledge the appeal (types feel legible), state briefly why types don't hold up (instability +
Barnum), and offer the HEXACO + method read instead — *"I can give you something more accurate and
actually actionable: a confidence-tiered read of how they actually communicate, and what to do with
it."* Then run the method.

## Preserved contradictions (do not launder into consensus)

1. Test-retest r≈.70 **underestimates** reliability (Gnambs) — it conflates dependability with real
   change.
2. LIWC dictionaries vs open-vocabulary ML is a **method shift, not agreement** — LIWC explains only
   ~5 % of trait variance; the field moved off it (observable-cues.md §1).
3. H–Agreeableness overlap: meta ρ≈.50 vs single-study .70 — instrument-dependent.
4. Dark-core ↔ low-H: r≈−.95 yet argued **distinct**; DT incremental validity over H is
   outcome-dependent and **unresolved**.
5. GFP and the "Big Two" metatraits: substance vs rater-bias artifact — the **artifact side is
   currently better-powered**.
6. Swearing→honesty **sign is contested**: positive on Feldman's Lie Scale, **negative on HEXACO H**
   (this skill's backbone) with more cheating (de Vries rebuttal) — do not use profanity as a clean
   honesty cue (observable-cues.md §3).
