# The method — the six-layer pipeline and the practitioner spine

The framework (frameworks.md) is the coordinate system; THIS file is where the practical
intelligence lives. Every layer outputs a **hypothesis with a confidence and a flip indicator**,
never ground truth. The layering is McAdams' three-level model (traits → characteristic
adaptations → narrative) with a practitioner front-end (baseline, observation) and back-end
(prediction, action).

## Why this method, not "score them on Big Five"

Four independent practitioner domains — negotiation, clinical case-formulation, intelligence
analysis, behavioral observation — converged on the **same operating spine** without borrowing
from each other. Convergence across unrelated fields is why the spine outranks any single
technique or framework:

1. **Baseline before deviation** — read the person against *their own* normal, not a population
   average.
2. **Competing hypotheses, not one story** — hold ≥2 explanations; prefer the least-disconfirmed.
3. **Cluster / triangulate** — the unit of signal is ≥2 co-occurring cues across ≥2 contexts,
   never a single "tell."
4. **Confidence is falsifiable and revisable** — every read names the indicator that would flip it.

The most empirically *discredited* practices (microexpression lie-detection, criminal profiling)
are exactly the ones that claim a **single cue has oracle power**. What survives scrutiny is
**process rigor under uncertainty**. Design every read around the spine, never around a tell.

## L0 — Baseline (precondition for everything below)

Before any read, characterize THIS person's neutral, low-stakes normal: resting pace, message
length, warmth, formality, emoji/punctuation habits, typical topics, response rhythm. Idiographic,
**not** population-normed (Navarro, *What Every Body Says*, 2008 — the logic is echoed in
surveillance detection and clinical observation, though the book is not itself a validated
instrument).

Why it is the precondition: **50–70 % of momentary Big-Five behavior is within-person state**
(Fleeson 2001, *JPSP* 80:1011). Today's message is one draw from a distribution — you cannot know
a draw is high or low without knowing the person's own mean and spread. Without L0, every
"deviation" you read is against a stereotype, not against them.

- Re-baseline over time — context, fatigue, relationship stage shift it.
- Track **variability itself** as a separate signal, not just the mean: some people have narrow,
  consistent distributions; others wide, context-sensitive ones. Emotional variability predicts
  neuroticism *beyond* mean level (Mader et al. 2023, *PNAS*) — log "how much they swing" and "how
  fast they recover" as distinct observations from "how negative they are on average."

## L1 — Observable signal (produces DATA, not conclusions)

Capture concrete, low-inference behavior **with the triggering stimulus attached**: what was said,
at what pace/register, in response to *what* (a question, a topic, a person). Navarro's rule: the
unit is a **cluster** (≥2 co-occurring cues pointing the same way), and a discomfort cluster is
only interpretable relative to the stimulus that immediately preceded it — work backward from the
cluster to the trigger.

- **No single-cue oracle claims** (see calibration-and-ethics.md §5 for the debunked list —
  microexpressions, "caps = angry," reply-latency → attachment). A lone cue is a lead, not a read.
- This layer is deliberately dumb: it logs "used three self-deprecating jokes when work came up,"
  not "is insecure." Interpretation waits for L2+.
- Cue→trait direction/strength lives in observable-cues.md — consult it, but remember every cue
  there is r<.25. You are collecting weak evidence to aggregate, not reading tea leaves.

## L2 — State/context adjustment (Gate G2)

Before attributing an L1 signal to disposition, ask: **is this today's mood/stress/role, or a
stable pattern relative to their baseline?**

- A single sample ≈ one occasion ≈ dominated by state. Single-occasion trait validity is r≈.00–.20
  (Diener & Larsen 1984, *JPSP* 47:871: single-occasion consistency ≈.00–.19; aggregated over
  weeks ≈.75–.95). **One conversation cannot yield a trait.**
- **Decision rule (G2):** trait ("this person is…") claims require **≥2 time/context-separated
  observations**. With one sample, downgrade to a **state reading**: *"in this message the person
  presents as anxious,"* not *"this person is neurotic."* Write it in state language.
- Neuroticism is the worst case — up to 69 % within-person variance; apparent negativity in one
  text is most likely transient. See G3.

## L3 — Trait estimate (Gate G3; HEXACO, confidence-tiered)

Only after multiple observations across contexts, place the person provisionally on HEXACO
dimensions — McAdams' "psychology of the stranger," acknowledged as **thin on its own**. This is
the layer most profiling stops at; do not stop here.

Attach a **confidence tier (A / B / C) to every trait** — the tier definitions, the E>C>>N
rationale, and the ceiling are the SOLE HOME of calibration-and-ethics.md §1 (R1); read them there
and apply, don't restate. In one line for use at this layer: **A** = Extraversion, dominance,
warmth (state with moderate confidence); **B** = Conscientiousness, Openness (hedge); **C** =
Neuroticism/anxiety, Agreeableness, Honesty-Humility, intelligence, creativity — **withhold or flag
near-unknowable from thin text.** Never phrase a claim above the stranger-level ceiling (~r.4).

## L4 — Values / attachment / motivation overlay (the *why*)

McAdams Level 2 — characteristic adaptations. Answers what a trait score cannot: what drives them,
what they protect, how they relate under stress. All are add-ons to, not replacements for, L3.

- **Schwartz values** (10-value circumplex; Openness-to-change↔Conservation,
  Self-enhancement↔Self-transcendence; cross-culturally robust in 70+ countries): read from what
  they *praise, criticize, sacrifice for, get defensive about*. Values = "what matters" (deliberate
  goals); traits = "how they act" (automatic) — non-redundant.
- **Regulatory focus** (promotion/gains/eagerness vs prevention/security/vigilance): the actionable
  communication lever — **regulatory fit** (gain-framing for promotion types, loss/security-framing
  for prevention types) reliably raises engagement. Detect by noticing which framing of the same
  ask gets more traction. (Trait *self-report* of regulatory focus is noisy; the *framing* effect
  is the solid part — use behavior, not a quiz.)
- **Attachment signature** — hyperactivating (anxious: reassurance-seeking, protest, hypervigilance
  to your cues) / deactivating (avoidant: withdrawal, minimizing, "don't need anyone") / flexible
  (secure: direct need-talk, quick repair) / oscillating (approach-avoid). **Critical caveat:**
  read this as *relationship-specific behavior under this relationship's stress*, NOT a clinical
  type. Self-report and clinical-interview attachment measures correlate only r≈.09 (Roisman 2007)
  — a chat-based read is a coarse behavioral signature, not an AAI classification. Attachment is
  **dimensional, not categorical** (Fraley taxometrics); avoid "they ARE an avoidant." Cross-check
  across relationships (Caron's "Russian doll": global vs domain vs relationship-specific layers
  can diverge — secure with friends, anxious with a partner).

Attachment/values add real variance beyond Big Five (attachment–neuroticism only r≈.49; Big Five
explains just 12–25 % of attachment) — this overlay is *not* repackaged traits.

## L5 — Interpersonal prediction (Interpersonal Circumplex + complementarity)

Place the read on **dominance × warmth** and use it to predict *dyadic* dynamics a solo score
can't:

- **Complementarity** (validated behavioral pattern): **dominance invites submission**
  (opposite/reciprocal), **warmth invites warmth** (matched), coldness invites coldness. Predict
  friction *before* it happens — dominant-vs-dominant clash, warm-vs-cold mismatch — and predict
  what will feel natural to elicit. (Warmth-reciprocity is robust; dominance-anticomplementarity
  moderate; the *behavioral* pattern is solid, its link to relationship *satisfaction* is not
  guaranteed — Cundiff 2015 found the pattern without the outcome link. Predict interaction, not
  happiness.)
- **Demand-withdraw** cross-check: is their conflict style pursue/demand or withdraw/stonewall, and
  does it line up with their L4 attachment signature (anxious↔pursue, avoidant↔withdraw)?
  Demand-withdraw is topic-contingent (whoever wants change pursues), not fixed by role.

### L5-dyad — 相性 (compatibility) reads: two dossiers → one dyadic forecast

When the ask is "profile BOTH of us and the 相性," run L0–L4 per person, then analyze the **dyad**
as its own object. The frame is the **Social Relations Model** (Kenny): any interpersonal behavior
decomposes into an **actor effect** (行為者効果 — what A brings to everyone), a **partner effect**
(パートナー効果 — what B elicits from everyone), and a **relationship effect** (関係効果 — what appears
ONLY in the A×B pairing). What people call 相性 is mostly the third — so a dyadic read that just
compares two trait profiles has missed its object. (Japanese term discipline: SRM's third term is
関係効果; attachment's relationship-specificity is 関係特定的; the ECONOMIC concept in §L6 is
関係特殊的投資 — three established terms, do not blend them into an ad-hoc coinage like 関係固有性.)

- **The hard ceiling (state it in every 相性 output):** relationship variance is largely
  **unpredictable from pre-interaction data** (Joel, Eastwick & Finkel 2017, *Psych Science* —
  "an earthquake, not chemistry"). A pre-meeting 相性 read is therefore a **meeting-viability +
  friction forecast + material inventory**, never a verdict on deep compatibility. Phrase it so:
  "運ぶ相性は高い / 深い適合は未確定 — 対面で決まる."
- **What you CAN read pre-meeting** (the forecast's legitimate content): shared concrete material
  (topics both engage, in their own words — the future callback inventory); values alignment or
  clash (L4 Schwartz per person); complementarity fit and structural friction (dominance×warmth
  pairing, pace/tempo mismatch); demand-withdraw × attachment pairing risk; regulatory-focus
  framing each person responds to.
- **What CREATES the relationship effect** (the two validated mechanisms — read for their raw
  material, and hand the *building* of them to the action-side skill): **perceived partner
  responsiveness** (feeling understood, validated, cared for — Reis) and **shared reality**
  (jointly held meanings, in-jokes, callbacks — Rossignac-Milon 2021). A dyad with rich callback
  material and mutual responsiveness signals has the raw material; whether it compounds is decided
  in interaction, not in the dossier.
- **Dyadic flip indicators** (name them like G5's): reciprocated question-asking, spontaneous
  self-disclosure deepening, future hooks offered by the OTHER side — each observed → upgrade;
  absent across meetings → downgrade to "fun but not compounding."

## L6 — What to do with it (Gates G5, G6)

The action layer — and where the read gets **tested rather than asserted**.

**Test the read live (tactical empathy — Voss; internally coherent practitioner method, not
RCT-proven, but adjacent to validated affect-labeling neuroscience, Lieberman 2007):**
- **Labeling** ("It seems like…", "It sounds like…" — never "I…"): externalize your hypothesis
  about their state so they *correct* it (wrong → free data) or confirm it (right → lowers charge).
- **Calibrated questions** ("What…/How…", not "Why…"): make them do the explaining; their answer
  *is* the read.
- **Mirroring** (repeat their last 1–3 key words): prompts elaboration, surfacing more of their
  model each pass.
- **"That's right" vs "you're right"** as a live falsification signal: "*that's right*" = genuinely
  felt understood (your formulation is accurate); "*you're right*" = placation/disengagement (stop
  talking) — treat it as evidence your read is OFF.

**Competing hypotheses — ACH (Gate G5; Heuer, *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, 1999):**
- Brainstorm **all** plausible explanations for a behavior cluster first — counter premature
  closure on the first tidy story.
- Work **evidence-row across hypotheses**, not hypothesis-by-hypothesis. Weight only **diagnostic**
  evidence — a cue "consistent with everything" discriminates nothing.
- **Select the hypothesis with the LEAST disconfirming evidence, not the most confirming.** This
  inversion is the whole point.
- Name the **indicator that would flip the read** (Heuer step 8) and the assumptions you're
  smuggling (Key Assumptions Check). ACH is a validated *debiasing* procedure (ICD 203); whether it
  beats unaided judgment on accuracy is mixed (Dhami 2019) — use it for the discipline, not a
  guarantee.

**Deliver as a case-formulation, never a diagnosis** (Eells; Johnstone & Dallos): a formulation
*explains* mechanism and is held as a working hypothesis expected to be partly wrong and updated as
more history arrives; a diagnosis *labels*. Use the 5 Ps as scaffolding when useful — Presenting
pattern, Predisposing, Precipitating, Perpetuating, Protective. **This is the single most important
import: read-as-hypothesis, never read-as-verdict.**

**Ethics (Gate G6) — argued as incentive dynamics, never as cheap moralizing.** Legitimate purpose
only (understand / communicate / self-protect); reversible, falsifiable language over fixed labels;
inferred traits treated as sensitive. When the user pushes on strategy, the justification is
**dynamic, not moral**: building relationship-specific surplus (関係特殊的投資 — Williamson's
asset-specificity term; shared reality, callbacks, mutual
investment) is exactly right — but converting it into leverage (holdup: "ここまで来たんだから",
narrowing their options, obligation-framing) teaches the other person that investing in the
relationship gets exploited, so they rationally **underinvest**, and the joint surplus dies. Keep
the asset, never the extraction — because extraction kills the asset. Details in
calibration-and-ethics.md §6.

**Where this skill STOPS (the READ/MOVE boundary):** this skill delivers understanding and
forecast — the dossier, the dyadic read, engagement *principles* (labels, calibrated questions,
regulatory fit). Concrete next-move coaching in a courtship funnel — what message to send, when to
ask someone out, date design, recovery from silence, 文例 — belongs to the action-side skill
(`courting-on-apps`, where installed). Hand the dossier to it; do not script the moves here.

## The proactive-relationship use (the primary goal)

For the center-of-gravity use — understanding someone faster and deeper to build a closer, healthier
relationship — three layers do the work: **L4** (frame requests in their regulatory fit and actual
values so they feel *met*), **L5** (defuse structurally-likely friction before it happens), and
**L6** labeling/calibrated questions, which simultaneously *test* the read and make the person feel
understood — a correct label lands as "that's right" and deepens rapport; a wrong one is corrected
and improves the read, so live-testing has no downside path. The **ethics gate (G6) keeps this
healthy, not braked**: a revisable hypothesis used to understand rather than control is what
separates intimacy from manipulation — a fixed covert label is both less accurate and less kind.
