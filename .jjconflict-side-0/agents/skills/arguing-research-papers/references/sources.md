# Sources — the provenance ledger (SOLE home) + the calibration inversion

> **Scope**: the ONE source-grade table for this skill. Every distilled rule was graded at capture
> time (grading after writing invites laundering). **Reflexive corollary** (the skill eats its own
> rule): this skill demands the author anchor every claim to a real, retrievable source — so its own
> claims are held to the same bar. Where an attribution is single-sourced, oral, or unverified, it is
> labelled here and **not laundered upward** into the body's voice.
>
> Grades: **author-confirmed** (the specific idea is vouched as the author's — *not* that a verbatim
> quote was retrieved) · **paraphrase** (named in the source; exact wording not retrieved) ·
> **third-party** (non-author commentary/summary) · **constructed** (engineered by this skill's
> synthesis; found in no single source) · **needs-verification** (named but exact content unretrieved —
> **DO-NOT-FABRICATE**: ship the named slot, never invent the items).

## §inversion — the calibration inversion (SOLE argument; SKILL.md carries the table)

Every source above corrected a **human novice's** failure: burying the contribution, failing to find
"so what", hedging into mush *or* overstating. A capable RLHF'd model fails **differently**, and the
prominence in SKILL.md follows the *model's* failure, not the human's.

The verdict from the model-failure analysis (author-confirmed introspective default behavior, grounded
against Toulmin's qualifier-to-grounds matching): **miscalibration is bidirectional and layer-split.**
- **Local-interpretive layer** — over-hedging **dominates by frequency**. RLHF politeness/harmlessness
  rewards non-committal language, so the default interpretation of a *real* result is drowned in stacked
  hedges.
- **Global-framing + evidence layer** — over-claiming / **fabrication dominates by fatality**. The model
  asserts unearned novelty ("first to", "novel", "significant" with no test), invents positioning
  ("unlike prior work"), refuses to commit (both-sides framing), and — worst — states evidence it does
  not have (fabricated citations/baselines/numbers).
- **Net reviewer risk**: over-hedging is more *frequent*; overclaiming + fabrication is more **FATAL** —
  a reviewer kills a paper on ONE fabricated cite or ONE unsupported "first to", whereas mushy hedging
  merely weakens.

Therefore the corrective is **never a global "be more/less confident"** — it is **per-layer**: assert
*toward strength* at the claim level (strip hedges the data licenses) and restrain *toward grounding* at
the framing/evidence level (kill unearned superlatives; anchor or placeholder every number). Made
first-class in SKILL.md: the **anti-fabrication HARD gate** (most fatal, most model-specific), the
**two-pass per-layer calibration**, **commit to one claim** (against both-sides sprawl), and
**MUST-NOT-FIRE** (against ceremony on trivial asks). This is why the skill leads with a fabrication ban
and a claim-calibration procedure, not with "tell a story".
