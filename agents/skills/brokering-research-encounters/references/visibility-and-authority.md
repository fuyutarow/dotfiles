# Visibility and authority — SOLE owner of broker boundary

This reference is the SOLE owner of field visibility, authority, actor authorship, upward rules,
and sibling cuts. Lifecycle meaning belongs only to `encounter-loop.md`. `AUTHORITY: NONE` means
the artifact creates no mandate, candidate, admission, decision, or scientific credit.

## Field matrix

| Field / artifact | Broker | Source | Recipient Director | Supervisor | Other sections |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| restricted raw locus/digest and raw content | locator/digest only; raw body only when separately designated declassifier | source-controlled | no raw body | NO | NO |
| consent decision, scope, revision, withdrawal history | record/relay | authors | scope needed to validate offer | NO | NO |
| declassified bounded encounter body and `MATCH_KEYS` | record/relay | supplies/approves by designated process | offered recipient only | NO body | NO |
| Director need locator/digest and required keys | exact-match interface only | no | authors | NO body | NO |
| `TECHNICAL_MEMO_OFFER` body | author/relay frozen offer | no | recipient only | NO | NO |
| local `HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT` / PULL evidence / admission record | canonical IDs plus locator/digest in receipt only | no | Director authors | NO body | NO |
| `ENCOUNTER_RECEIPT` identifiers/outcome | author terminal close | may inspect within scope | may inspect within scope | aggregate only | no |

The Supervisor receives no raw method, need, or offer body. At most declassified aggregate counts
with no scientific credit may travel through an existing `SECTION_SIGNAL`; the broker does not
author a new signal. Raw content is neither globally indexed nor upward visible.

## Actor-author rules

| Act | Sole author | Broker may do | Broker must not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| consent / withdrawal | human source or its authorized recorder | bind declared evidence | infer consent |
| declassification decision | source-designated declassifier | bind the exact approved fields/digest | infer approval or widen approved content |
| need and required keys | Director | exact-match interface | interpret need or choose meaning |
| `ENCOUNTER_RECORD` | broker | freeze record and loci | alter raw source evidence |
| offer | broker | create one recipient-bound offer | broadcast, rank, or create candidate |
| `HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT` as PULL evidence, ADMIT/REJECT/DEFER | recipient Section Director | verify canonical PULL key and cite existing actor/grant loci/digests | author, amend, or decide any of them |
| `ENCOUNTER_RECEIPT` | broker | close from exact owner evidence | counterfeit human or Director act |
| candidate/test/RUN/LEARN | existing section roles | none | own or trigger scientific work |

`AUTHORITY-RECEIPT` is the declared sixth evidence archetype: evidence is an exact artifact join
plus an act authored by the actor who owns it. Helpers may relay or read-only validate but cannot
counterfeit human consent, a Director need/pull/admission, or scientific `LEARN`. Trust only
loci/digests plus owner evidence; a worker `PASS` is opinion.

## Locality, non-blocking, and sibling cuts

Broker-local: restricted raw evidence, consent/declassification bindings, exact-match decision,
offer lifecycle, and terminal receipt. Section-local: pulled `HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT`, all admission,
WIP/axes, candidates, runs, and learning. Never reuse `SECTION_TRANSFER_PACKET`, subscription,
delivery, or transfer-commit semantics; no committed-learning bus receives this material.

No participant waits for acknowledgement, quorum, all recipients, Supervisor, verifier, or a
global join. No-adoption, withdrawal, expiry, rejection, or deferral blocks neither source,
recipient, nor unrelated work.

- `systematizing-knowledge` owns corpus position; this skill owns only encounter-to-artifact-to-
  receipt routing.
- `directing-research-sections` owns pulled `HUMAN-METHOD-INPUT`, local admission, runs, `LEARN`,
  and the committed-learning bus.
- `supervising-research-programmes` owns programme work and remains body-blind here.
- `governing-research-documentation` owns document locus and lineage, not semantic admission.
- `orchestrating-agents` owns actors/dispatch, not this semantic map.
- `forging-novel-theses` owns candidate genesis; an implementation/platform owner owns any future
  runtime.

Modal operation is solo, zero helpers. Read-only validation may fan out under the generic
`orchestrating-agents` contract, but cannot decide a human or Director act. **No harness → same
map, serial.**
