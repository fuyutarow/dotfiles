## §A — The four lenses (深さ・広さ・構造・時間): the DEFINITION layer

**The book's 4 axes are 4 LENSES you ROTATE THROUGH, NOT a MECE partition.** The book applies all four to
醤油 SIMULTANEOUSLY (深さ on 原料の質, 広さ on 酸化・環境, 構造 on the MECE 分解, 時間 on 5年後の課題) — it never
claims the four carve content into disjoint buckets. On their native content the lenses LEAK: 因果(causality),
ボトルネック, and システム思考 each belong to more than one lens by the book's own text. **MECE is IMPOSED by the
skill-supplied OWNER RULES in §A.5, not inherited from the book.** §A.5 gives EVERY shared concept exactly one
owner rule, proving the leak set complete (so no lens claims another's content).

Each lens is made operable by fixing (a) its UNIT of analysis, (b) one diagnostic question, (c) a crisp
boundary against its neighbors, and (d) a HIGH cell that REQUIRES a citable observation artifact. **A lens
whose HIGH cell needs no observation is decorative — it is cut.** The diagnostic questions below
(どれくらい具体的/多面的/簡潔/道筋立っているか) are **skill-supplied / needs-verification** — they appear in
NEITHER the deck nor the book digest (they were previously mislabeled author-confirmed). The lens
construction does not depend on their exact wording; if the physical book confirms different verbatim
one-liners, swap them in (§C.6).

The lens UNITS, stated once:
- **深さ** = ONE phenomenon drilled DOWNWARD (vertical).
- **広さ** = the SET of SIBLING candidates listed SIDEWAYS (horizontal).
- **構造** = RELATIONSHIPS among already-surfaced elements; adds NO new elements.
- **時間** = how elements CHANGE / FLOW (dynamics).

### A.1 深さ DEPTH — vertical, into ONE thing

- **Unit:** ONE phenomenon / cause / method, examined downward.
- **Q (skill-supplied; needs-verification):** 「どれくらい具体的か？」
- **Definition (author-confirmed):** 原因・要因や方法（解決策）の具体化・掘り下げ — how far down you have
  traced ONE thing toward its mechanism. The author's central claim: 4視点の中でも『特に「課題の深さ」が
  足りない』 — depth is the most important and most-lacking axis for the book's HUMAN audience (see §C.4 for
  why this bias inverts for an agent).
- **OWNS:** concretizing abstract→specific; the 症状(symptom) vs 病因(root-cause) split (the medical metaphor:
  あなたは顧客の医師 — 表に出ているものは『症状』、注力すべきは『病因』); the Why-so? chain drilling ONE causal
  line to mechanism (EXECUTION protocol in §D.3); 固有名詞・数字・メカニズム as the evidence of depth.
- **LOW tell:** abstract subject + vague verb ("users struggle", "the auth is broken"); 5W1H not statable.
- **HIGH tell (requires citation):** the symptom AND the root-cause LINE/identifier named — e.g. you can point
  to the exact line that CAUSES the behavior, with a number/mechanism.
- **Boundary:** depth descends ONE branch. The moment you start comparing sibling branches, you are in 広さ;
  the moment you weigh which fix moves the most, you are in 構造 (see A.5 bottleneck rule).

### A.2 広さ BREADTH — horizontal, across siblings

- **Unit:** the SET of sibling candidate causes / factors / approaches / stakeholders, examined sideways.
- **Q (skill-supplied; needs-verification):** 「どれくらい多面的か？」
- **Definition (author-confirmed):** 考慮する原因・要因やアプローチの多様性・多面性を確保すること — enumerating
  the alternatives you have NOT yet considered so you do not tunnel on the first explanation.
- **OWNS:** enumerating alternative causes / options / stakeholders; **system-thinking and stakeholder maps as
  the ENUMERATION instrument** (the 広さ slice of システム思考 — see A.5(6) for the feedback-loop slice that
  belongs to 時間); the **per-node breadth check** that interlocks with depth — NAMED here (A.6), its EXECUTION
  owned by §D.3.
- **LOW tell:** the first explanation taken as the only one ("competitors? there are none").
- **HIGH tell (requires citation):** ≥2 candidates, EACH ruled in or out by a cited check — you can name what
  you eliminated and on what observation.
- **Boundary:** breadth only LISTS the siblings. Grouping them and ranking their importance is 構造, not 広さ.

### A.3 構造 STRUCTURE — relationships among what depth+breadth surfaced

- **Unit:** the RELATIONSHIPS among elements already surfaced by 深さ and 広さ. Adds NO new elements.
- **Q (skill-supplied; needs-verification):** 「どれくらい簡潔か？」
- **Definition (author-confirmed):** 『深さ』『広さ』で見えてきた要素を意味のあるまとまり（カテゴリ）に分割し、
  要素間の関係性・相対的な重要性を把握すること — arrange, group, rank.
- **OWNS:** grouping into MECE categories; the logic tree; relative-importance ranking; the static dependency
  ARRANGEMENT a system map produces (A.5(6)); AND the **highest-leverage bottleneck AMONG already-surfaced
  candidates** (which fix moves the most) — see A.5.
- **LOW tell:** a long, tangled, flat list with no priority; an over-long explanation is itself a tell that
  structure is missing.
- **HIGH tell (requires citation):** elements grouped MECE + the leverage point named, explainable in one
  breath. A multi-element structural claim DECOMPOSES into per-element sub-claims, each needing its own
  citation (N-for-N) — "stop at one citation" (§C.1) means one-per-blocking-claim, not one grep for the whole
  tree.
- **Boundary:** structure is the ATEMPORAL arrangement; the temporal cut is the operational discriminator in A.5(4).

### A.4 時間 TIME — how elements change and flow

- **Unit:** how elements / states CHANGE and FLOW over time.
- **Q (skill-supplied; needs-verification):** 「どれくらい道筋立っているか？」
- **Definition (author-confirmed, verbatim sense):** 経時変化・因果関係、物事のプロセスや流れ（道筋）を捉える
  こと.
- **OWNS:** temporal / process sequence; before→after; the path / roadmap; dynamic (time-indexed) causality —
  A-over-time→B, feedback loops (the 因果ループ / システム思考 dynamics slice, A.5(6)).
- **LOW tell:** no sequence, no path, no "what happens next."
- **HIGH tell (requires citation):** an ordered process + a concrete next-step path, each step grounded
  (N-for-N, as in A.3).
- **Boundary:** see the causality split (A.5(2)) and the reorder/feedback discriminator (A.5(4)) — only
  TIME-INDEXED causality lives here; a static root-cause is 深さ, a static relative-weight map is 構造.

### A.5 The six owner rules (hard; the SOLE home of every shared-concept disambiguation)

The lenses leak on their native content; these six rules IMPOSE the MECE partition by giving each shared
concept exactly one owner. They are stated once, here. The set is complete — every concept that appears under
two lenses in the book is resolved below.

**(1) ボトルネック.** The book mentions ボトルネック inside the 深さ chapter as the *motive* for drilling (you
go deep to find where the leverage is), NOT as 深さ's deliverable (this positioning is
**needs-verification**). Owner rule:

> **root-cause of ONE phenomenon = 深さ; highest-leverage AMONG already-surfaced candidates = 構造.**

Finding *why X happens* is depth. Deciding *which of the surfaced things to fix first because it moves the
most* is structure. Never file "which fix has the most leverage" under 深さ.

**(2) 因果 (cause) — tripartite (skill-supplied).** The book's verbatim 時間 definition bundles 因果関係
UNQUALIFIED ("経時変化・因果関係…"), so by the author's text causality is filed WHOLESALE under 時間. That is
too coarse for an agent that must decide which command to run, so this skill splits "cause" into three senses
and assigns each to one lens:

| Sense of "cause" | Lens | When |
|---|---|---|
| snapshot-down: root cause of ONE thing, now | 深さ | "why does THIS line fail?" |
| atemporal relative-weight map | 構造 | "of these causes, which dominates?" |
| time-indexed: sequence / process / feedback | 時間 | "A over time drives B; the loop is…" |

> **Output rule: state WHICH sense of "cause" you mean before you say "cause."**

**(3) MECE分解 / 分割.** The ACT and the RESULT split across two lenses:

> **the ACT of splitting one thing into candidates = 広さ (enumeration); the RESULTING grouping / ranking of
> already-surfaced elements = 構造.**

**(4) プロセス / 流れ — the operational reorder discriminator (replaces the failed "indexed by time" test,
which mis-fired on value chains).** Run this RUNNABLE check:

> **構造:** "Does REORDERING the elements change the meaning? **NO** → atemporal → 構造" — even if drawn
> left-to-right (e.g. a static value-chain map: 生産→加工→販売 read as an arrangement carries no before/after
> dependency).
> **時間:** "Does the agent need a BEFORE/AFTER, or a feedback loop where state at t feeds t+1? **YES** → 時間.

Resolved value-chain case: a static value-chain map = **構造**; the SAME chain when reasoning about a
bottleneck PROPAGATING over time = **時間**.

**(5) 関係性.** Atemporal vs feedback:

> **atemporal relationships (grouping, dependency-as-arrangement) = 構造; relationships that are feedback loops
> where state at t feeds t+1 = 時間.**

**(6) システム思考.** The book files システム思考 under BOTH 広さ (as an enumeration instrument — the 広さ chapter)
AND 構造 (因果関係や時系列…主にシステム思考が応用できる — the 構造 chapter), so it is a genuine shared concept and
needs its own owner rule:

> **システム思考 as STAKEHOLDER / option ENUMERATION = 広さ; the static dependency ARRANGEMENT it produces
> (grouped, ranked, no t→t+1) = 構造; the FEEDBACK-LOOP / causal-loop DYNAMICS it surfaces (因果ループ図,
> state at t feeds t+1) = 時間.**

This is why A.2 says system-thinking powers the ENUMERATION slice of 広さ, not 広さ wholesale — the
arrangement it yields is 構造 and the loop dynamics are 時間 by the same t→t+1 test as (4)/(5).

### A.6 The per-node breadth interlock (NAMED here, EXECUTED in §D.3)

Depth and breadth are not sequential phases; they INTERLEAVE per node. At EVERY Why-so node, before
descending, run a one-line 広さ check: *"what sibling causes am I skipping, and why this branch?"* Pure depth
without per-node breadth produces "drilled deep on a branch I never validated" — a distinct failure from the
top-level "deepened the wrong problem" (§C.2). The interlock is NAMED here; its EXECUTION (where it sits in
the Why-so ladder, the root-state rule that opens the tree) is OWNED by **§D.3**.

### A.7 Lens labels are revisable hypotheses

Any lens assignment is provisional. You cannot know whether a causality is static-or-dynamic until you
inspect (run the A.5(4) reorder test on observed structure, not a guess); so if inspection reveals it is
dynamic, re-file from 構造 to 時間 and continue. Do not treat the first label as a one-shot gate — the lenses
are a diagnostic for where thinking is thin, never a classification you must get right before looking.

## §B — 課題 vs 解決策: the two-object dimension (SOLE owner)

This is the SECOND dimension and the source of the order/priority rules. SKILL.md states the "problem-first"
rule as a one-liner and points here; §A only cross-references this section.

### B.1 Two objects, each with its own resolution

課題(problem) and 解決策(solution) are TWO distinct objects, each carrying its OWN resolution on all four
lenses. The author-confirmed priority:

> **解決策よりも課題が大事** — because **課題の重要度以上の価値は生まれない** (a solution can be no better than
> the IMPORTANCE of the problem it answers; verbatim — keep 重要度).

**Cardinal anti-pattern (author-confirmed):** 課題の解像度が低いまま解決策に飛びつく. For a coding/research
agent this is the #1 error — writing code, proposing a refactor, or picking a library BEFORE inspecting what
the problem actually is. Most "speculation" failures are this anti-pattern wearing a solution costume.

The §1 table is "4 lenses applied ASYMMETRICALLY across {課題, 解決策}", problem first — **NOT a symmetric 4×2
partition**; the 解決策×深さ cell is INTENTIONALLY THIN (B.3).

### B.2 Order (enforced in SKILL.md's gate)

```
raise 課題 resolution  →  validate it is worth solving (§B.5)  →  only then raise 解決策 resolution
```

Lead with 深さ on the 課題; lead with 広さ→構造→時間 on the 解決策 (the asymmetry, B.3). Never invert.

### B.3 The depth-asymmetry (owned here, single-homed)

The author says all four lenses apply to both objects, but the agent's depth-BUDGET is asymmetric: **深さ
spends FIRST on the 課題 side**; the 解決策 is sharpened mainly with 広さ→構造→時間.

- **課題:** depth-first — drill to the 病因 / mechanism.
- **解決策:** breadth-first — enumerate approaches, then structure, then sequence.

**解決策×深さ is thin BY DESIGN — a skill-supplied BUDGETING decision, not a claim that the book omits 解決策
depth.** The survey CONFIRMS the book gives 解決策 a genuine deepening mechanism — 解決策の高解像度化 via
**How を繰り返す** (repeat How? to a runnable unit), 複数の解決策候補を広く知る, 新規性・自社の強み・代替比較,
実験と検証 — i.e. How-deepening IS depth on the solution. So the thinness is OURS, not the book's: this skill
spends the depth budget on the 課題 FIRST, and treats 解決策×深さ as non-empty only when concretizing the
MECHANISM of ONE already-chosen solution (the How-repeat drill). The §1 table marks this cell INTENTIONALLY
THIN so no reader mistakes the table for a balanced 8-cell partition. What remains **needs-verification** is
only the chapter-STRUCTURE question (does Ch6 give 解決策 a full standalone 深さ chapter or fold How-deepening
into 広さ・構造・時間), NOT whether 解決策 has depth at all — it does (§C.6).

### B.4 良い課題の3条件 (problem-quality framework; needs-verification; DO-NOT-FABRICATE)

Problem-quality is NOT reducible to a single バーニングニーズ test. The book gives a named Ch4 framework,
**良い課題の3条件**, as the criteria for a good 課題. The section is confirmed to EXIST, but the three items
themselves are **needs-verification** — the survey does not give the verbatim three. **DO NOT FABRICATE the
three conditions.** The imperative when a user needs the actual items: **flag that the verbatim three require
book Ch4 verification, and OFFER the safe partial** — problem-quality is a multi-criterion judgment of which
バーニングニーズ is ONE component, not the whole (§C.6, open question). Hand the user that partial rather than
inventing the list.

### B.5 The バーニングニーズ gate

Run this BEFORE deepening, so depth is not spent on a problem nobody has.

- **Author-confirmed core (use this as the test):** the mere EXISTENCE of a problem ≠ a burning need — a
  バーニングニーズ is one the customer 今すぐ何としても解決したい (urgent, acute, strong); 今すぐ何としても解決したい
  かどうか が判定軸. (The book's positive claim: solving a burning need cascades to the upper課題 above it —
  上位の課題も連鎖的に解決される.) **Note (paraphrase, verbatim NOT in survey):** the often-quoted
  『「課題がある」と「すぐにお金を払ってでも解決したい」の間には大きな差がある』 captures the SENSE but the
  お金を払ってでも phrasing is not confirmed in the survey — do not present it as the author's exact words
  (§C.6).
- **Operational (a)/(b) criteria — THIRD-PARTY, labeled as such, a heuristic not an author quote:** (a)
  someone already spends time / money / effort on it; or (b) leaving it unsolved blocks the goal / business
  continuity / compliance. Useful as a checklist; do NOT attribute it to 馬田.
- **Scope note:** problem-SELECTION (which problem to pick) is UPSTREAM and not this skill's job; this skill
  owns problem-COMPREHENSION but runs this gate so depth is not wasted on the wrong problem. If the gate fails,
  broaden (広さ) or re-select before deepening.

### B.6 Research mapping

For a research agent: **課題 = research question / gap; 解決策 = method / experiment.** Same order (sharpen the
question before the method), same depth-asymmetry. This skill stops at sharpening your OWN grasp of ONE
question before acting — **multi-paper synthesis is owned by `systematizing-knowledge` / sok**, hand off there
(the cardinality cut: single local artifact → here; a CORPUS synthesized into a position → sok).

### B.7 The minimal イシュー note

安宅『イシューからはじめよ』 is positioned as complementary by 3rd-party readers (イシュー = problem-SELECTION
upstream; 解像度 = problem-COMPREHENSION downstream). This relation is **reader-supplied, not the author's
claim** — keep it to one line: *problem-selection is upstream of this skill.* Do not import イシュー machinery.

## §C — Limits / epistemics (SOLE owner)

Honest guardrails. Every OTHER site (SKILL.md §0/trigger/procedure, §D below) states each of these as a
one-line imperative and points HERE; this is the only place they are argued. The localization rule: no
re-argument elsewhere.

### C.1 The stop condition — stop at 『十分』, not maximal (SOLE owner of the rationale)

The author has a built-in guardrail against analysis-paralysis: the deck explicitly says **『解像度を上げる
ことに時間を使いすぎない』** and advocates acting on **『十分な』(SUFFICIENT)** resolution, not maximal. This
skill operationalizes 十分 as the **citation stop-condition**, identical to the load-bearing gate inverted:

> **Fire at ZERO citations on a blocking claim; STOP at ONE.** Equivalently: stop once one more observation
> would not change WHICH action you take.

No global "am I sufficient overall?" judgment is needed — it is per-claim and countable. A multi-element
structural/temporal claim is N blocking claims (one per grouped element), so it needs N citations, not one
(A.3 N-for-N): "stop at one" means one-per-claim, never one-per-tree. "Raise resolution forever" is a
MISREADING the book itself forbids, not a flaw of the book.

### C.2 The six failure modes — what this skill must NOT become

1. **INFINITE DEEPENING / perfectionism.** Cured by C.1: one citation on the blocking claim closes it.
2. **DEEPENING THE WRONG PROBLEM** (top-level) — and its per-node twin **DEEPENING THE WRONG BRANCH**. Guards:
   the バーニングニーズ/イシュー gate (§B.5/B.7) + top-level 広さ ("right problem?") AND the per-node 広さ
   interlock (§A.6 / executed §D.3, "right branch at each Why-so node?") + the root-state rule (§D.3). These are
   two distinct failures, two distinct guards.
3. **行動なき情報収集 / box-ticking — RATIONALE only (the RULE is owned by §D).** The loop is 情報×思考×行動 — a
   PRODUCT; reading/probing endlessly without deriving 洞察 and ACTING is the dead form. The deck warns:
   単に現場に行って意見を聞くのではなく、事実を基に自分で考えて洞察を得ること. The four lenses are a DIAGNOSTIC for
   where thinking is thin, never a 4-checkbox ritual. **The RULE — "the loop must close on an action" — is
   stated as LAW once, in §D.1; this clause owns only the epistemic rationale and points there.**
4. **RITUAL APPLICATION / OVER-FIRING.** See C.4 — for a capable model this is the DOMINANT risk. Do not run
   the lenses on a trivial, already-grounded task; if the blocking claim already has a citation, resolution IS
   sufficient — assert and move. Re-diagnosing is the failure.
5. **RE-BRANDING CLASSIC TOOLS AS NOVELTY** (既視感 / repackaging) — see C.5.
6. **A BOOK SUMMARY.** Every retained line must change a tool call. Test each against *"does this make the
   agent run a different command?"* If no, cut.

### C.3 The 情報×思考×行動 fidelity note (so the loop is not mistaken for "think harder")

Author-confirmed formula: **解像度 = 情報(収集) × 思考 × 行動** — a PRODUCT. If any factor is zero the product
is zero, so 思考 is co-equal, NOT worthless. Confirmed author phrase: **『行動することで解像度は間違いなく
上がる』**. The strong slogan 『行動なくして解像度は上がらない』 is **paraphrase, not a quote** — do not present
it as the author's words. The book's depth-raising is a 内化⇄外化 cycle (intake ⇄ externalize); that is the
author's own central concept, mapped to agent actions in **§D.2**.

### C.4 Calibration — over-application is the DOMINANT risk for THIS consumer (inverted from the book)

The book corrects HUMAN under-deepening: 『特に課題の深さが足りない』, so it systematically pushes readers
*deeper* and under-warns about over-deepening. **A capable model's default failure is the REVERSE** —
over-elaboration, over-structuring, ritual application on already-grounded tasks. Therefore:

| | Book's human audience | This skill's agent consumer |
|---|---|---|
| dominant error | under-deepening (課題の深さ不足) | OVER-firing / over-elaboration on grounded tasks |
| corrective bias | "go deeper, act more" | "stop at one citation, don't re-diagnose" |
| what to make prominent | the deepening 型 | the MUST-NOT-FIRE list |

This is why SKILL.md's MUST-NOT-FIRE / over-application defense is FIRST-CLASS and prominent, not an appendix.
The skill inherits the book's substance but FLIPS its calibration for the consumer.

### C.5 The 既視感 / repackaging honesty (the sharpest external critique)

The sharpest fair critique of the book is that its core is classic logical thinking in a fresh metaphor —
『核となるのはロジカルシンキングや既存の分析手法』; for readers who already internalized 照屋『ロジカル・
シンキング』 or 細谷『具体と抽象』 it can feel 既視感. Honest framing the skill must adopt:

- Why-so? / なぜなぜ powers **深さ**; MECE / logic-tree powers **構造**; system-thinking powers the enumeration
  slice of **広さ** (its arrangement = 構造, its loop dynamics = 時間, per A.5(6)); causal / process analysis
  powers **時間**. These are SUBSUMED tools, not inventions.
- The genuine contribution is (i) the unifying **4-lens vocabulary**, (ii) the **"which lens is thin?"**
  diagnostic, and (iii) — added by THIS skill — the agent-specific **anti-speculation CITATION GATE**.
- **Say so.** Do not market subsumed tools as novelty.

### C.7 The acting-on-hypotheses boundary + the SEQUENCING handoff rule (SOLE owner)

## §D — The acquisition MECHANISM (the HOW layer; SOLE owner of every acquisition RULE)

This is the layer that turns "stop guessing" into "go look." It folds in the previously-orphaned HOW content
(formerly pointed at a non-existent `action-loop.md`; now single-homed here). **Every acquisition RULE is
stated as LAW exactly once below; §0/§C and other sites one-line-point here.**

### D.1 The action ladder — cheapest-first, the only allowed evidence (OWNER)

The loop = 解像度 = 情報(収集) × 思考 × 行動, a PRODUCT (fidelity in §C.3). For a coding/research agent the
SCARCE factor is ground truth, so the loop biases toward INSPECTION. The ladder is the rung order, cheapest
first; take the LOWEST rung that turns the unknown into an observed fact, then re-check the gate:

```
grep/glob  →  read file/source  →  run / probe / log / measure  →  reproduce n=1  →  web-fetch  →  ASK
```

- **Rationale for cheapest-first (skill-supplied):** each rung up costs more time / context / human
  attention; spending a dear rung when a cheap one would have grounded the claim is waste, and ASK (dearest)
  burns the user's attention — earn it by exhausting the cheaper rungs. SKILL.md §0 enumerates this ladder as
  one bare line and flags "rationale in reference.md §D"; this is the rationale.
- **NEGATIVE-EVIDENCE LIST (never a rung; owned by SKILL.md §0, restated for completeness):** "in my
  experience" / "usually" / "this kind of library typically" / "probably" / "should be" /
  たぶん・おそらく・はず・一般的に. None of these grounds a claim.
- **THE LOOP-MUST-CLOSE-ON-AN-ACTION RULE (LAW, owned HERE):** **行動なき情報収集 is a banned dead form — the
  loop must close on an ACTION** (a fix shipped, a probe run, a recommendation given, a smallest experiment
  launched), and acting yields new primary info → re-enter at the gate. §C.2 #3 owns only the epistemic
  rationale for why endless intake is a failure and points here for this rule.

### D.2 内化 ⇄ 外化 — the kata table, by FUNCTION (no contested 型-numbering)

The book's mechanism is the 内化⇄外化 cycle (intake ⇄ externalize) rerun fast. The deck/cover mention "48 of
型" but never enumerate them, so this skill maps kata to concrete agent actions BY FUNCTION, with NO normative
numbering (§C.6):

| Function | Author's kata (内化 / 外化) | Concrete agent action |
|---|---|---|
| **内化 — intake** | 現場に行く / カスタマーマニア | reproduce the bug locally; run the real workload; read the actual data |
| | インタビュー（意見でなく事実） | ASK the user/issue for the FACT (repro steps, exact input), not their opinion of the cause |
| | サーベイ（多数事例） | grep the codebase / search issues & commits for every occurrence of the pattern |
| | 一次情報をとる | read the source / the spec / the API definition itself, not a summary of it |
| | 語彙・概念を増やす | name the precise mechanism (e.g. "N+1", "TOCTOU") — see D.5 |
| **外化 — externalize** | 言語化（ツリー化で深さを可視化） | write the Why-so? tree / the claim with its citation inline |
| | 人に教える / コミュニティ対話 | state the hypothesis 明確・簡潔・ユニーク back to the user; the gap you cannot articulate is the gap to inspect |
| | プロトタイプにして表現 | write the smallest probe / failing test / MVP that EXTERNALIZES the guess as a runnable check |

The cycle: externalizing (writing the tree, stating the hypothesis, building the probe) EXPOSES the next
ignorance, which sends you back to intake. The diagnostic test for "high enough": can you state the hypothesis
**明確 かつ 簡潔 かつ ユニーク**? If not, a lens is thin — go look.

### D.3 The Why-so? / 症状-vs-病因 protocol — with per-node 広さ EXECUTION and the ROOT-STATE rule

Depth (§A.1) is executed by the Why-so? chain; the medical metaphor governs it (あなたは顧客の医師 — the
surface is 症状, drill to the 病因). For an action plan, swap Why? for **How?** ("最初の一歩がはっきりするまで
How? を自問自答する"). The execution discipline:

- **ROOT-STATE rule (LAW, owned HERE) — at the empty tree, 広さ runs FIRST.** Before committing ANY drill
  direction, enumerate ≥2 candidate causes from ONE cheap observation, so depth never drills the first cause
  that came to mind. This is the exact guard against the §3 N+1-guess failure (the agent that "knows" it's an
  N+1 before measuring). Empty tree → breadth first, then descend.
- **PER-NODE 広さ EXECUTION (the A.6 interlock, executed) — at EVERY node, before descending, ask:** *"what
  sibling causes am I skipping, and why this branch?"* Each branch you take must be the one a cheap
  observation justified, not the one that was salient. This closes the "drilled deep on an unvalidated branch"
  failure (§C.2 #2).
- **Each Why-so step is a citable claim**, not a story: the move from node to child must be grounded by an
  observation (a line Read, a measurement), per the gate.

### D.4 n=1 — the single-case drill

A single, fully-inspected case beats many shallow ones: reproduce ONE failure end-to-end, instrument it,
follow it to the 病因. n=1 done thoroughly surfaces the mechanism that aggregate guessing never will. (Author:
一事例にこだわって分析することで本当の Why に辿り着く。)

### D.5 語彙 — concepts are the resolution of perception

「言葉や概念を知っていることで、より正確に事象や感情を分割し、的確に理解することができる」 (author's example:
自信 → 自己効力感 self-efficacy / 自尊心 self-esteem). For an agent: naming the precise failure class
("N+1", "race", "off-by-one", "TOCTOU") sharpens both the search (you know what to grep for) and the fix.
More precise vocabulary = finer分解 = higher resolution. Increase 語彙 deliberately.

### D.6 The single worked 起業家 ladder (the canonical depth drill)

The author's own worked example of the Why-so? chain, end to end — note that an 安直な解決策 surfaces midway
and is REJECTED because the 病因 is not yet found:

```
「起業家は困っている」                                       ← low-res (何にでも「そうですね」)
  └→「起業家はお金に困っている」
       └→「起業家は資金調達に困っている」
            └→「起業家はシード資金の調達ができなくて困っている」
                 └→「シード資金調達のための情報が手に入らなくて困っている」
                      └→「その情報が検索エンジンで引っかからなくて困っている」   ← 病因 reached
```

Midway, the tempting 安直な解決策 「情報を提供すれば良い → Web メディアを作ろう！」 appears — REJECTED:
*安直な解決策は大抵機能しない。その課題が生まれて残っている理由・構造を把握する必要がある.* Only after the 病因 is
reached does a real hypothesis form: 「SEO に資源を激振りした資金調達支援ページ」. The agent analogue: do not
"add caching" at "the API is slow" — drill (and run the root-state breadth check at the empty tree) until the
病因 is a cited line, THEN choose the 解決策 that targets it (the §3 before/after exemplar in SKILL.md is this
ladder applied to code).
