## §1 — Argument logic (the a-temporal skeleton a reviewer checks)

**Toulmin's six elements** — lay bare a single inference to find the joint a skeptic attacks:

> **Claim** (the conclusion) ← **Grounds/Data** (the facts appealed to) via **Warrant** (the general
> inference-licence, "since …"), where the warrant rests on **Backing** (field-dependent authority,
> "on account of …"); the claim carries a **Qualifier** ("probably / necessarily") and is subject to
> **Rebuttal** ("unless …").

Use it to locate *which joint* a reviewer will deny — usually the **warrant** (the load-bearing,
unstated assumption the author thinks is obvious) or the **rebuttal** (the unhandled counter-example).
State a warrant *iff* a competent reviewer of THIS venue might not grant the leap; leave field-standard
warrants implicit (spelling them out condescends and wastes the page budget). **Schema is universal;
BACKING and QUALIFIER are filled with the venue's currency** (`reconciliation.md` §4).

**Craft of Research (Booth-Colomb-Williams) — three tools:**
- **Topic → Question → Significance** template: *"I am studying [TOPIC], because I want to find out
  [QUESTION], in order to help my reader understand [SIGNIFICANCE / the larger consequence]."*
  Inability to complete clause 3 means you still have a **topic, not a problem** — the single most
  common motivation failure. This *is* the G1 instability+cost slot.
- **The five-element argument** (recursive — audit any paragraph or the whole paper by it): **Claim +
  Reasons** (you generate them) **+ Evidence** (external, reader-accepted fact — the anchor) **+
  Acknowledgment-and-Response** (anticipated objections) **+ Warrants** (link reason→claim). "You can
  think up *reasons* but you cannot think up *evidence*" — the reason/evidence conflation is a model
  failure (asserting inference in the register of fact).
- **Problem = Condition + Cost**: a research problem is a *destabilizing condition* (something unknown/
  misunderstood) **plus a cost** ("so what?"). Unfolds in the intro as: shared Common Ground →
  Destabilizing Condition ("but/however") → Cost → Solution (the claim). Cost has two currencies —
  practical (applied venues) vs conceptual (theory/math); pick by venue, never fake practical payoff
  for conceptual work.

## §2 — Narrative / structure frames (the persuasion-zone spine)

All of these are **persuasion-surface** tools (title/abstract/intro/discussion) and govern *salience
and order*, never the facts — the zone-split (`reconciliation.md` §1) bounds every one.

- **OCAR** (Schimel) — **O**pening (stable context, the funnel) → **C**hallenge (the gap/tension =
  the engine of attention) → **A**ction (what you did/found) → **R**esolution (the answer + broader
  "so what", closing the loop back to the Opening, with interpretation **deferred to the widening
  Discussion** — the *hourglass* shape: broad → the narrow waist of Methods/Results → broad again). The
  default whole-paper arc when a draft reads as a flat list. Variants Schimel catalogues: **LD/LDR**
  (Lead-Development[-Resolution]: answer first, the journalistic form — use when the reader scans for
  the conclusion, i.e. abstracts and ML PC review), **ABDCE** (fiction; rarely).
- **ABT** (Olson) — "[setup] **AND** [more setup], **BUT** [the problem/contradiction], **THEREFORE**
  [consequence]." One sentence, one pivot. Both a drafting kernel and a diagnostic: compress the paper
  to one ABT sentence — *if there is no honest BUT, there is no story yet.* The **narrative spectrum**:
  **AAA** ("and, and, and" — a boring expository list) → **ABT** (the single-pivot sweet spot) → **DHY**
  ("despite, however, yet" — an over-narrated tangle). Push a draft toward the ABT center: add a BUT to
  an AAA list; prune competing buts from a DHY tangle. Caution: the **THEREFORE must be
  evidence-licensed** (`reconciliation.md` §1); use ABT for the *top-level framing message only* and
  still report all results (including mixed ones) in the record-zone Results.
- **C-C-C** (Mensh-Kording) — **Context → Content → Conclusion**, applied *self-similarly* at three
  scales: paper (intro/results/discussion), section (orient/body/close), paragraph (topic sentence /
  body / conclusion-to-remember). The last sentence of the *context* block states the gap. **Here**
  it is the structure that carries the CLAIM at paper/section scale — an argument-level frame, not a
  prose fix. The paragraph-level instance (topic-sentence-first, no zig-zag) is executed as an audit
  by `linting-prose` (L3); reuse it by pointer, don't re-run it here.

## §3 — The introduction (where most papers are won or lost)

**CARS (Swales) — Create A Research Space** is the load-bearing intro frame (the G3 hinge, the build
procedure's step 3). Three moves: **Move 1 Establish a territory** (claim centrality *with evidence*,
not "since the dawn of time"). **Move 2 Establish a niche** — the "but/however": counter-claim /
indicate a gap / raise a question / continue a tradition. **Move 3 Occupy the niche** (announce the
present work, its contributions, the structure). The funnel is right but **its mouth must be specific**
("the specific active conversation this paper joins", not the widest true statement about the
universe). **Move 2 is the hinge — cut it against NAMED prior work** (the G3 artifact; the fabrication
ban on the citation, `calibration.md` §6).
- **Escalation** (Alvesson & Sandberg): a *gap* is the reviewer-legible within-paradigm niche
  (gap-spot AND tie it to a consequence). To *redirect a field*, **problematize** a named assumption —
  but still translate the challenge into a concrete, evaluable gap or it reads as untestable polemic.

**The questions every intro must answer** — Widom (five points), the Heilmeier Catechism, and
Mensh-Kording converge on ONE checklist; use it to *fill the CARS moves*, not as four separate
templates: (1) what is the problem — *jargon-free*? (2) why does it matter / **who cares** (the
reader-cost)? (3) why is it hard / why do naive approaches fail? (4) why is it still open — what is
wrong with prior solutions? (5) what is your approach + result? (6) how is success measured (the
yardstick)? Have the contribution articulated by **the end of page 3**. (These are field variants of
one checklist — not a reason to write four.)

**SPJ's distinctive moves** (Peyton Jones, CS/ML — the parts not in the checklist): **one key idea**
("one ping"); **contributions as a refutable bulleted list** with forward references (§4); **related
work LATE** so it does not smother the idea (`reconciliation.md` §3); and *do not recapitulate the
journey* — present the distilled idea, examples before the general case.

## §4 — The abstract & title as distinct genres

The abstract is **the argument in miniature**, not a summary — its claim strength must equal the
Results, no wider (`calibration.md` §4). Pick the template by venue:

- **Nature "here we show" summary paragraph** (broad general-science): (1) one field-intro sentence
  comprehensible to *any* scientist (no jargon); (2–3) background + the gap/rationale; (1) the main
  result, signposted "Here we show …"; (2–3) key results; (2–3) broad-context implication. ~200 words.
- **SPJ four-sentence abstract** (CS/ML): (1) the problem; (2) why it is interesting; (3) what your
  solution achieves; (4) what follows. No background padding.
- **Structured / IMRaD-labeled abstract** (biomedical, trials, systematic reviews): explicit
  Background/Objective · Methods · Results · Conclusions; primary outcome first in Results; mandated by
  reporting standards (CONSORT/PRISMA).
- **Composite arc** (genre-neutral): Context → Gap → "here we show" → Findings → Implication, each once.

Choose the *form* by venue (structured for scan-by-label evidence syntheses; unstructured narrative for
flagship/CS); choose *quantified vs qualitative* by genre (empirical/ML must quantify the headline;
math/theory states the theorem/qualitative advance, deferring constants to the body). All of these are
zone-split-bound: the abstract framing is persuasion; the numbers in it are record.

**Title craft** — a **declarative result-title** ("X Improves Y") wins only when the result is single,
strong, and the whole point across the claimed scope; otherwise a **mechanism/scope title** ("A
[method] for [problem] under [condition]") that states true scope without prejudging (`calibration.md`
§4). For method papers, a **coined method name** dissolves the keyword-vs-brevity tension (it is both a
unique keyword and a brand). Ban praise-adjective titles ("A Novel, Efficient …").

**Contribution bullets** (CS/ML venue convention — the *list* is a genre choice, the *hierarchy* is
the law, `reconciliation.md` §3): "Our contributions are: (1) …", each item = one claim + a pointer to
the section/table/theorem that establishes it, ordered by importance, 3–4 items. The list doubles as
your self-audit that every claim has an anchor. Where the review form has no Contribution field and one
editor reads deeply, **embed** the contribution in prose instead.

**Adjacent editor-facing genres** (argue what the manuscript body cannot): the **cover letter**
(sentence 1 = the result and why it matters across communities / flagship fit; then context/gap, key
finding, why-this-venue, competing-interest + suggested/opposed reviewers) and the **significance
statement** (e.g. PNAS's is a short non-technical word cap [venue-fact — re-verify]). These are maximally editor-register assertive
(`reconciliation.md` §3, editor-vs-reviewer).

## §5 — Cross-cutting tools

- **Contribution type (Michael Black — "Novelty in Science").** Reviewers conflate novelty with
  technical *complexity*, so a simple idea reads as "not novel". State explicitly **which kind** of
  contribution you claim — new **problem** / **insight** / **method** / **data** / **empirical result**
  — so the reviewer applies the right yardstick, and frame simplicity as a feature ("surprisingly, X
  suffices"). This is the G1 contribution-type slot.
- **Assertion-evidence captions & headers (Alley).** Lead each figure caption and each section opener
  with a **full-sentence assertion** stating the take-away ("Method X reduces error 30% across all
  three datasets"), then the evidence — not a topic label ("Results on CIFAR"). Eliminate ambiguous
  referents ("this", "it") and vague quantifiers so a reviewer cannot read a weaker/stronger claim than
  intended. (The *wording* of the caption is `linting-prose`; *which claim the caption asserts* is here.)
- **Resource / artifact papers (dataset · benchmark · software · model · corpus).** A distinct
  contribution whose value is *a reusable OBJECT the community adopts*, not *a belief changed* — so the
  McEnerney value-reset re-points: the reader-cost is **what they cannot currently do / measure without
  this**, and the claim gains object-specific slots the belief-frame omits — **coverage /
  representativeness**, **access & format** (how to obtain and use it), **license** (reuse terms; an
  unclear or restrictive license kills adoption), **maintenance / versioning** (who keeps it alive), and
  **reuse evidence** (baselines run on it, early adoption, comparison to existing resources). Novelty
  here = "no existing resource covers this need," not technical complexity; position (CARS Move 2 still
  applies) against the nearest EXISTING resource by a **coverage / access / quality** gap.
