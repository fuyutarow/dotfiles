# Programme design

## SOLE owner: programme-altitude problem, frame, formulation, and portfolio meaning

This reference migrates the creative research design loop at programme altitude. It decides the
research question, frame portfolio, and programme transition. It never chooses a section method,
candidate, protocol, run, local test, or live-section instruction; those belong to
`directing-research-sections`. Corpus position, present anomalies, premise excavation, thesis
generation, expensive one-bet testing, finished claims, documentation, continuity, and control
plane route to their named sibling skills below.

## Earliest-unsatisfied stage diagnosis

Name one earliest unsatisfied dependency and route sequentially. A route is a handoff, not
programme authorship of the sibling's work.

| Stage | Evidence condition | Route / programme output |
|---|---|---|
| `corpus-unclear` | field position or target-agnostic donor relation is unbounded | `systematizing-knowledge`; receive bounded position |
| `anomaly-unverified` | observation may be noise, leakage, or artifact | `raising-resolution`; receive cited observation |
| `assumptions-unexposed` | a dominant frame has unexamined premises | `surfacing-blind-spots`; receive blind-spot packet |
| `problem-underconstructed` | topic/frame lacks target, comparator, horizon, distribution, tolerance, or residual | construct `PROBLEM_LANDSCAPE` here |
| `thesis-missing` | selected frame has no candidate slate | publish/maintain `OPEN_ISSUE`; an independent Director bids; after `SECTION_MANDATE -> SECTION_CHARTER -> GROUNDING_PACKET`, the Section Director may route local candidate genesis to `forging-novel-theses` |
| `candidate-selection` | frozen local slate awaits admission | `directing-research-sections` |
| `one-bet-untested` | one selected claim needs an action | Section Director applies the cost cut; `acting-on-hypotheses` or executor |
| `program-steering` | two or more independent bets need allocation/reopen | decide portfolio here |
| `finished-claim` | evidence is ready for an argument | `arguing-research-papers` |

`governing-research-documentation`, `continuing-long-running-tasks`, and `orchestrating-agents`
respectively own documentation authority, continuity transport, and bearer/visibility control.

The Programme Supervisor never invokes `forging-novel-theses` for a live issue and never receives
the candidate batch. Candidate output remains local to the granted Section Director.

## Frame construction and honest residual

Construct a `PROBLEM_LANDSCAPE` with a **CONTROL**, a **PREMISE-BREAK**, and an **ORTHOGONAL** frame.
The latter two must alter a premise, decisive relation, or discriminator; route labels alone do not
make frames distinct. Preserve the open-set residual: unknown alternatives are not erased because a
three-slot slate exists. When an alternative cannot be honestly formed, record `COVERAGE_GAP` with
the attempted transformation, fixed constraint, and impossibility witness. Never fabricate a frame
to fill a portfolio slot.

## Formulate what a programme issue can decide

For every live issue state: Goal `OBJECTIVE_ID`, `SUCCESS_OBSERVABLE_ID`, target relation; comparator;
horizon; scaling regime; distribution/access assumption; tolerance; decisive relation; a cheap victory (how an apparent success could miss the research
question); held-out witness/trust firewall; what formalization discards; resource/safety limits;
and one success/kill/reopen observable. The held-out witness may not be selected on the optimized
metric. These are programme interfaces, not instructions for a method or protocol.

## Dominance and invalidation topology

Every landscape names each dependency as `UPSTREAM_INVALIDATOR`, `EXISTENCE_TEST`, or `DOWNSTREAM` and
records its dominated work. `PROGRAMME_DECISION` alone releases portfolio-level upstream dominance and
issue eligibility after its linked receipt. It never releases a Section's within-mandate escalation.
Spare capacity never releases work and never changes the axis. An axis change requires a signer
amendment/supersession of the Goal Constitution followed by a new snapshot revision.

Inside an already Programme-released mandate, the first admissible action is one minimal
existence/discriminator test. `SECTION_DIRECTOR_COMMIT` alone releases full sweep, scale, or GPU port
from that test's linked receipt. When both a portfolio block and a local escalation block apply, BOTH the
Programme release and the later Director release are required. Fixed parameters or seeds are execution,
not SEARCH.

## Multi-bet steering

Before allocating, state the independence basis: differing premise, target, discriminator,
evidence/access path, or failure mode. Named routes sharing these are one correlated bet. Preserve
a stable core plus a bounded atypical probe; novelty alone is not value. Compare information per
resource under the declared envelope, not raw activity or optimistic narrative.

| Decision | Programme criterion |
|---|---|
| `persist` | predeclared observable reduces a live uncertainty within constraints |
| `pause` | dependency/access/constraint blocks interpretation without supporting closure |
| `retire` | bounded family has a supported negative closure; do not retire the open world |
| `reopen` | controlled surprise, frame break, or accepted request/recommendation invalidates the current issue boundary |

`SECTION_REOPEN_REQUEST` and `AUDIT_RECOMMENDATION` can supply declassified reasons to consider
reopen. Neither enacts it; only a new `PROGRAMME_DECISION` does.

## Scientific progress accounting

The programme North Star is goal-aligned, current-grounded, nonduplicate, minimally discriminating,
measurement-valid receipt-linked LEARN/hour. `learnPerHour` is primary and `searchPerHour` is a leading
measure only when the receipt can become that LEARN. A growing receipt backlog is not success.

At programme altitude, count SEARCH only from a declassified terminal `PROOF_RECEIPT`, `RUN_RECEIPT`,
or `KILL_RECEIPT` backed by tracked, nonignored evidence. A discriminating null, falsification,
negative, or KILL receipt counts exactly like a positive receipt. Count LEARN only from a distinct
Director commit consuming one of those receipts. Replays, packet splits, trivial nondiscriminating
runs, and learning prose without that commit count zero. `EXACT_BLOCKER` remains a blocker, not a
SEARCH receipt.

A bid, mandate, charter, candidate, agent, document, prompt, token, freeze, normalization, audit brief,
continuation, verifier call, smoke case, or instrumentation check is CONTROL. Report such counts
separately. A proposal-only interval—even 100 minutes—is `SEARCH_NOT_STARTED` and
`LEARN_NOT_STARTED`, not progress. `.agent-state` is control/continuity state, never research evidence.

Use bounded declassified windows. Compare `searchPerHour`, `learnPerHour`, grounding latency,
known-duplicate rejection, invalid-measurement rate, stale-work count, scale-release count,
receipt-to-commit completion, latency, ready-slot idle time, utilization, and exact blocker/wait counts before a
portfolio allocation. Never request raw receipts to explain an aggregate.

Utilization is diagnostic only. A measurement `FAIL` or `UNKNOWN` creates an instrumentation-repair
item and contributes neither SEARCH nor LEARN.

Cross-section packet publication, delivery, replay, admission, and adoption remain separate propagation
metrics and never enter SEARCH/LEARN numerators. Programme steering may use bounded aggregate delivery
lag, unrouted counts, topic coverage gaps, conflict alarms, and programme-visibility violations. It may
not request a packet body, premise delta, method, subscription, recipient decision, or acknowledgement.
