# Constructing and selecting research problems

> **Sole home**: construction, comparison, and selection of research problems. Generating a thesis for
> one selected problem is `forging-novel-theses`; testing one expensive/irreversible selected tree is
> `acting-on-hypotheses` while a cheap deterministic reversible probe goes to the domain/plain executor;
> excavating hidden premises/tacit constraints in one received frame is
> `surfacing-blind-spots`; inspecting the present artifact behind a frame is `raising-resolution`.

## 1. Expose before constructing

Do not treat an inherited topic or dominant frame as the search space. If no frame exists, use grounded
evidence to state exactly one `PROVISIONAL-CONTROL`; do not create an alternative slate yet. Then obtain
a Blind-spot packet from `surfacing-blind-spots` with:

- typed load-bearing assumptions;
- an open-set residual;
- human-tacit answers or the honest token `UNELICITED`;
- a two-level depth trace;
- a strategic stop reason.

This packet is not a new problem slate. It exposes what the slate must vary.

## 2. Construct before ranking

An inherited topic is not yet a research problem. A usable frame states:

```markdown
- Object / population / regime:
- Observed or predicted relation:
- Competing account or missing comparison:
- Discriminating observation:
- Consequence if resolved:
- Provenance:
```

Attempt three role slots with different functional roles:

- **CONTROL** — holds the dominant premise;
- **PREMISE-BREAK** — breaks or replaces a load-bearing assumption;
- **ORTHOGONAL** — changes another assumption type or pursues the open-set residual.

After one concrete attempt, a role may be `COVERAGE GAP` only when its record names the attempted
transformation, the fixed fact/constraint, and why a fabricated frame would be illegitimate.

Recipes may include:

- controlled anomaly;
- incompatible explanations;
- blocked consequential decision;
- constraint inversion;
- missing baseline / counterfactual / regime;
- an admitted structural-transfer route, whose donor relation is first bounded by
  `systematizing-knowledge` and whose correspondence is then built by `forging-novel-theses`.

Do not count paraphrases or recipe labels as candidates. Two frames are distinct only if their
held/broken premise, decisive relation, discriminator, or consequence differs.

`directing-research` may admit or decline the route because it owns program allocation. It must not
write the source-to-target map: `systematizing-knowledge` owns donor discovery without a target mapping,
and `forging-novel-theses` owns the mapping or a `MAPPING-BREAK`. On return, preserve every frozen attempt
in a path-and-SHA-256-bound `TRANSFER DISPOSITION`. A source result may justify testing but cannot support
target adoption or retirement; those require a frozen `TARGET RESULT` whose candidate ID, observation,
prewritten threshold, exact locus, and threshold result are checked against the transfer bundle.

## 3. Consequence before method fluency

For each frame, state what becomes possible, impossible, or falsified **before** sketching a method.
Then assess attackability.

Method fluency is not importance. A quickly generated method may indicate a well-posed opportunity, a
crowded template, or mere familiarity. Check crowding and consequence directly; do not reward or punish
fluency itself.

## 4. The fresh lever is an attackability claim

A fresh lever is a named change in instrument, data access, method, cost, institutional access, or
conceptual representation that changes what can be learned now.

```markdown
- Previously blocked because:
- New lever:
- Evidence the lever exists now:
- Which discriminator it makes reachable:
- Shelf trigger if the lever is not ready:
```

The lever does not establish importance or novelty. It establishes only a plausible present line of
attack. If building the missing instrument is itself the research contribution, state that explicitly.

## 5. Selection axes stay separate

Record at least:

| Axis | Operational question |
|---|---|
| consequence | What decision, capability, or theory changes if resolved? |
| discriminability | Can reachable observations separate this frame from alternatives? |
| feasibility | Are access, time, and methods sufficient for a meaningful test? |
| novelty delta | What changes relative to the nearest known prior? |
| bounded loss | What is the maximum cost of learning enough to update? |

Do not use a universal product or sum. Multiplication hides which weak axis killed a candidate and
implies unsupported commensurability. A local threshold is allowed if the axes and rejection reasons
remain visible.

## 6. Selection is fallible

Idea evaluators can overweight feasibility, underweight originality, or penalize unfamiliar proposals.
Therefore:

- freeze and deduplicate the batch before ranking;
- preserve each candidate's axis-level record;
- declare the required separation, frozen evidence surface, hostile lens, and acceptance condition for
  an independent evaluation pass;
- distinguish evaluator preference from evidence of truth;
- compare the proposal-stage judgment with execution-stage artifacts.

`orchestrating-agents` owns the actor, visibility, veto, and authority assignment that instantiates this
domain requirement. Selection may admit more than one direction into a portfolio. Admission is not
validation.

## 7. Exploration allocation and stop rule

Do not copy the excavation budget here. Point to the `Search budget` inside the Blind-spot packet, then
allocate only the optional cross-frame thesis probe:

```markdown
- Blind-spot excavation: [packet locus / Search budget]
- Cross-frame thesis probe: [NONE, or N frames × M candidates with hard cap]
```

Problem search can become avoidance. Stop constructing when the cross-frame cap is reached, or:

1. CONTROL, PREMISE-BREAK, and ORTHOGONAL roles exist as actual frames or honest `COVERAGE GAP`
   records;
2. the important axes have explicit evidence or an `UNKNOWN`;
3. at least one frame has a reachable discriminator;
4. one bounded attempt at an unused legitimate assumption type adds no new premise break, decisive
   relation, or discriminator—or yields an impossibility witness.

Then hand the selected frame to `forging-novel-theses`. If no frame has a reachable discriminator,
report the access/tool gap or shelf the program with a trigger; do not manufacture a thesis to hide the
gap.
