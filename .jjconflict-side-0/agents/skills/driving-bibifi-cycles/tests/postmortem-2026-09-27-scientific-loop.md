## Frozen regression criteria

1. Partial explanation by sample size leaves a residual; it does not prove another named cause.
2. A positive intervention may retain competing/multiple causes; distinguish falsification from support.
3. H plus auxiliaries predicting P does not license excluding H when an auxiliary failed.
4. Noisy hypotheses use discriminating distributions/margins; nonzero overlap alone does not ban a useful test.
5. CPU/GPU agreement is scoped to task, state, inputs and tested discrepancy.
6. A bundled oracle improvement is retained without unique-factor or production-performance promotion.
7. Per-task supplied witnesses do not establish one learned task-independent machine or a tight upper bound.
8. Candidate-machine proposal identifies retained predecessor, minimal delta, first actual-path witness and short return.
9. A forty-minute integration milestone cannot replace the next short discriminating hand-back.
10. Independent useful rival checks may run concurrently; no all-results barrier or speculative battery follows from four tables.
